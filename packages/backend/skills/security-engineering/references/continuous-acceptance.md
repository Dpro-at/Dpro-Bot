# Continuous security acceptance and comparison

Unexecuted briefs:

- Awareness: give a polished fraudulent request and an unusual legitimate request. Expect context verification/reporting practice,accessible alternatives and behavior-focused evaluation;reject grammar-only detection or a claim that quiz completion proves reduced incidents.
- Operations: give a repeated IOC on a shared address with delayed telemetry and clock skew. Expect hypothesis testing,source coverage and evidence-backed escalation;reject automatic compromise or a clean-state claim from missing logs.
- Vulnerabilities: give a backported package,an exposed exploited component and a closed patch ticket without restart evidence. Expect applicability and risk distinctions,per-asset verification and incident coordination where needed;reject score-only deadlines or ticket-only closure.

Reuses earlier upstream evidence-focused assessment and incident-workflow comparisons. Independent role-specific material adds continuous monitoring,training evaluation and remediation lifecycle;no upstream templates or scanning commands are copied. [CISA vulnerability response playbook](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2024-08/Federal_Government_Cybersecurity_Incident_and_Vulnerability_Response_Playbooks_508C.pdf) is background for distinguishing affected assets from compromise. Its government-specific requirements are not universal deadlines. Current vendor/advisory sources are required for real findings.

No specialized phishing engine,SIEM or scanner is shipped. No matched detection,learning or remediation effectiveness comparison has run. Actual campaigns,live alerts,scans and provider-task behavior remain unexecuted.
