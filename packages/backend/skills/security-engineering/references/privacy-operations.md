# Privacy operations and assessment

## Establish purpose and applicability

Identify processing purpose,data categories,people affected,organization role,locations,recipients and lifecycle. Distinguish what policy says from observed practice. Use supplied records and current primary legal/regulator sources for a real jurisdictional question;do not infer applicability or an exemption from organization size alone.

Map collections,uses,sharing,retention and rights-request systems,including backups,vendors and derived data. Identify children,sensitive data or automated-decision context where actually relevant without inventing facts. Legal interpretation and risk acceptance belong to the responsible authorized reviewer;this workflow supports evidence and implementation planning.

## Assess practices and gaps

For each applicable requirement or policy commitment record its source/date,scope,current control,evidence and gap. Distinguish an unknown control from a confirmed failure. Review transparency,minimization,access/correction/deletion workflows,consent or other relevant basis,and vendor/transfer arrangements as required by the actual regime. No single checklist covers every jurisdiction.

For a rights request,resolve identity and scope through the approved process without collecting disproportionate extra data. Track systems searched,exceptions and response state. A deletion action in the primary database does not establish deletion from every recipient or backup;document actual limits and authorized retention obligations.

## Plan implementation and review

Produce a processing map,requirement-to-practice register and prioritized remediation plan with owners and dependencies. Distinguish product changes,operational procedures,policy wording and legal decisions. Editing a notice does not make a contradictory practice compliant. Keep precise citations/effective dates verified or explicitly unresolved.

Data export,deletion,vendor contact,policy publication and external notification require actual user authority. Use synthetic examples in portable artifacts and restricted case records for real personal data. State evidence and legal-review limits;do not certify compliance,anonymization or absence of privacy risk from a document review.
