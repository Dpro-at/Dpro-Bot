# Security awareness training

## Define the behavior

Identify audience,work context,language/accessibility needs and the actual risky decision to improve. Use relevant incident patterns or approved policy,with private details removed. Separate general awareness,role-specific instruction and a measured behavior change. Completion of a slide deck does not prove that people can act correctly under pressure.

Choose a small number of observable learning outcomes,such as reporting a suspicious request through the approved channel or checking an unusual payment change through an independent route. Avoid fear-based claims,stereotypes and blame. Give learners a practical action and a way to obtain help,not just a list of threats.

## Build realistic learning

Create scenarios that reflect actual workflows without reproducing sensitive messages or exposing personal data. Explain relevant signals,uncertainty and safe responses. Do not teach that every typo proves phishing or that polished writing proves legitimacy. Include cases where the correct answer is to verify context or report uncertainty.

Sequence explanation,worked example,practice and feedback. Tailor privileged-user training to actual administrative decisions rather than recycling general employee material. Provide accessible text alternatives and clear instructions. Reuse course-design or learning-support where available,but keep security-specific evidence and escalation pathways explicit.

## Evaluate and improve

Use a scenario or observable task to assess the intended behavior,with a stated rubric. Record baseline,participation and limitations before interpreting results. Quiz performance,click rate,reporting rate and real incident reduction are different measures;do not infer causality from a single campaign. Avoid punitive public ranking or collecting unnecessary personal behavior data.

Phishing simulations,tracking links,impersonation messages and employee campaigns need separate organizational authorization,privacy review and operational scope. This role does not automatically send tests to staff. Deliver an editable training packet,facilitator notes,practice,feedback and follow-up plan. Actual training delivery and behavior change remain separate acceptance.
