# Security audit and assurance

## Define the engagement

Identify objective,criteria,system boundary,period,audience and assurance expectations. Distinguish an internal control review,a readiness assessment,a technical test and an accredited certification engagement. Confirm access to evidence and any independence/conflict constraints. Selecting this role does not confer certification authority.

Translate applicable criteria into a control/evidence map with real owners and expected operation. Reuse existing compliance-evidence resources when available,but retain the specific security test purpose. A policy statement describes intended design;implementation evidence and operating effectiveness require different support.

## Plan and test evidence

Define population,sampling method,period and exceptions before drawing conclusions. Explain why the selected sample supports the requested claim and its limits. A screenshot of one setting cannot establish year-long operation across all assets. Missing evidence can be a limitation or a control gap depending on the criterion;do not silently mark it passed.

Trace evidence to system/source,date,scope and relevant control. Corroborate interviews with records or observation where needed. Inspect whether evidence is complete,stale,contradictory or produced by the very control being assessed. Protect restricted artifacts and avoid copying secrets or excessive customer data into workpapers.

## Evaluate and report

Document condition,criterion,cause where supported,impact and recommendation for each exception. Separate evidence-backed findings from improvement suggestions and unresolved questions. Give management an opportunity to correct factual errors without erasing the audit trail or weakening supported conclusions.

Deliver a scoped report,control-test workpapers,exceptions and remediation ownership. State criteria/version,evidence period,sample limits and any assurance qualification. Verify closure with appropriate new evidence rather than a promise or ticket status. Regulatory filing,public attestation,certification and production remediation are separate authorized actions. A clean sampled review does not establish universal security.
