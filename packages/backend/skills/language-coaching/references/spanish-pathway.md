# Spanish lesson pathway

Start with the learner's intended variety,register,purpose and available modality. Spanish-speaking contexts differ in vocabulary,address forms and usage. Preserve a requested regional context rather than treating one default as universally correct. Maintained library resources remain English;live practice follows the learner's request and host policy.

## Task and starting evidence

Use a short meaningful attempt and supplied curriculum to choose support. Keep comprehension,writing,interaction and actual audio evidence separate. A learner may recognize a tense or form without being able to choose it independently in conversation. Select a manageable communicative objective rather than importing a rigid level-to-grammar list.

Provide enough model/input to attempt the task,then let the learner respond. Choose relevant vocabulary,agreement,reference,time/aspect or another structure because it serves the intended meaning. Adapt depth to observed difficulty and avoid overwhelming a beginner with every conjugation or regional alternative.

## Contextual grammar and variation

Explain the specific contrast in the actual sentence. Do not treat a permanent-versus-temporary slogan as a complete explanation of copula choice,or a list of trigger words as a complete mood rule. For narration,make the time frame and perspective clear. Verify uncertain lexical forms,inflections,pronoun patterns or collocations with a trustworthy reference.

Address forms and their associated verb usage depend on region and relationship. The RAE/ASALE [reference on the regional pronoun vos](https://www.rae.es/dpd/vos) supports recognizing legitimate variation;it does not settle every local register choice. Do not mark an established regional form wrong solely because it differs from the tutor's preferred paradigm.

Spelling and accent marks can matter to meaning or the selected writing target. Handle a likely keyboard slip proportionally without a blanket ignore-all-accents rule. A text-only exercise cannot establish pronunciation or listening. If audio/transcription is actually used,consider recognition uncertainty before attributing an error to the learner.

## Feedback and transfer

Distinguish a meaning-changing error,a valid variety choice,a stylistic alternative and an uncertain interpretation. Follow the agreed accuracy/fluency balance. Offer a focused correction or hint and check a fresh related use when assessing learning. Repeating the supplied correction is assisted practice,not proof of general mastery.

Use the shared progress record under authorized retention. Do not infer native language or identity from a possible false friend,save private responses globally or activate reminders automatically. Named exam practice follows supplied/current official criteria without issuing an accredited rating. Deliver useful observed progress,a concrete next attempt and the exact modality/reference limits.
