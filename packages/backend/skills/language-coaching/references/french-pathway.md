# French lesson pathway

Identify the requested variety,register and purpose before choosing examples. French used in a daily conversation,a formal message and a specific exam task can have different expectations. Preserve the learner's chosen context rather than treating one region's usage as the only valid French.

## Establish a useful task

Use a short meaningful attempt to observe comprehension,production and available vocabulary. Do not infer one global proficiency band from a typed sentence. Choose a communicative target such as asking for information,describing a situation or comparing options,then select the language needed for it.

For a beginning task,provide manageable phrases and enough explanation to use them. For a more developed task,choose a relevant contrast in reference,time,agreement,pronouns or register. A curriculum may supply a sequence; otherwise adapt from the learner's actual evidence rather than importing a rigid level-to-grammar list.

## Form and context

Noun-group agreement can be taught as a connected pattern:the relevant noun,determiner and adjective forms belong to the same phrase. Check the specific construction and exceptions before stating a rule. The OQLF's [noun-group agreement reference](https://www.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/francisation/ordres_prof/capsules-grammaticales/accord-groupe-nominal.aspx) is a starting reference,not proof of every agreement or variety case.

Distinguish what a spelling contrast communicates from what may sound similar. Do not claim to have heard pronunciation in text. Accents and endings can matter to meaning or written accuracy; handle a keyboard slip proportionally without declaring that all accent differences must be ignored. When spoken and formal written conventions differ,identify the task/register instead of assigning a universal error label.

For past narration,pronouns,auxiliary choice or mood,use a specific verified context and explain the actual contrast. Avoid blanket mnemonics that hide construction-dependent behavior. Verify uncertain inflections,collocations and lexical meanings with a trustworthy reference. A lookalike word in another language is a cue to check meaning,not permission to assume the learner's native language.

## Lesson loop

Give an appropriate model or hint,then let the learner produce a response. Prioritize a useful correction and ask for a fresh related use. Accept valid regional or stylistic alternatives. In conversation,follow the agreed feedback timing so the task remains interactive; in writing practice,make the selected accuracy target visible.

Track what the learner did independently and what depended on a prompt or supplied answer. A repeated correct form demonstrates that attempt,not automatic long-term mastery. Use varied retrieval where appropriate and choose review load from availability rather than a universal quota.

## Limits

Exam practice uses actual supplied/current official criteria when requested; do not issue certified ratings from chat. Text-only work does not assess actual listening or pronunciation. Keep private responses out of portable settings by default and persist notes only under the host's authorized data policy.
