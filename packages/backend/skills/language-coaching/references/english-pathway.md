# English lesson pathway

Establish the learner's actual use: everyday interaction,reading,writing,work or a named curriculum/exam task. Ask about the desired variety/register only when it changes the lesson; preserve a supplied British,American or other valid English context. Do not infer nationality or native language from an error pattern. Maintained resources remain English and learner practice follows the host/request language policy.

## Diagnose the target skill

Use a short relevant attempt. A learner may understand a complex text while needing support to produce a question or coherent paragraph. Keep comprehension,accuracy,interaction and fluency evidence separate. Select a manageable target from the observed task rather than assigning a fixed grammar sequence from a broad level label.

For beginners, a useful lesson can focus on a practical statement/question and the necessary vocabulary,word order and form. At a more developed level, work on time/aspect,reference,collocation,connected explanation or register as the task requires. These are examples of design choices,not an official CEFR grammar syllabus or a requirement to teach every listed topic.

## Meaning before a rule slogan

When teaching time/aspect,make the intended time frame and speaker perspective clear. Contrast a completed past-time account with a present-connected situation using verified examples appropriate to the variety. Do not reduce all usage to a keyword list or one universal translates-to-tense rule. The British Council's [present perfect reference](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/present-perfect) supports the basic contrast; verify more specific usage or variety differences when needed.

For articles,countability,prepositions and verb patterns,use the actual phrase and context. Some combinations are lexical rather than predictable from the learner's first language. Teach a useful pattern with a fresh application,not an exhaustive exception list. A stylistic alternative is different from an ungrammatical form. Do not rewrite every valid sentence into the tutor's preferred voice.

## Practice and feedback

Provide a brief model or contrast,let the learner attempt a related task and choose feedback that serves the accuracy/fluency goal. Keep intended meaning and accept valid alternatives. A contraction,regional spelling or informal phrase may be appropriate in one task and unsuitable in another; explain the actual register rather than marking it globally wrong.

Use a new context to check transfer. Distinguish a copied correction from independently choosing the form. For reading/writing,check textual evidence and coherence. For text-only conversation,do not claim pronunciation,listening or real-time speaking quality. If actual audio is available,consider speech-recognition uncertainty before attributing an error.

## Domain and assessment boundary

For an exam or specialist document,inspect the supplied/current official task criteria rather than inventing score rules. An informal lesson observation is not an accredited score or global language rating. Record the practiced skill,variety,support and uncertainty through the shared progress template. Preserve only authorized learner data; do not activate a review schedule or global memory overlay automatically.
