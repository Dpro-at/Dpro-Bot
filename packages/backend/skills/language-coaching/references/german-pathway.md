# German lesson pathway

Establish the learner's intended variety,register and use. Standard written German,a local spoken context and a named exam/curriculum can emphasize different evidence. Preserve the learner's goal and do not replace it with a universal syllabus or a demand for one accent.

## Diagnose and choose the task

Use a short relevant attempt to identify the next useful support. A learner may recognize complex clauses but need practice producing a simple request. Keep comprehension,writing,interaction and actual spoken evidence separate. Choose a manageable communicative target and the vocabulary/structure that enables it.

When a noun is introduced,include useful lexical information such as article and relevant plural where it supports the task. Verify the exact form rather than treating a suffix mnemonic as exception-free. Use phrase-level examples so case,preposition,verb and meaning remain connected.

## Clause structure

Teach finite-verb placement through the actual clause type. In an ordinary main declarative clause,distinguish the second constituent from the second word; a fronted phrase can contain several words. The Goethe-Institut's [basic grammar reference](https://www.goethe.de/resources/files/pdf141/appendix-ii-27.01.181.pdf) provides an introductory illustration. Questions,imperatives,subordinate structures and more complex verb clusters require their own context; do not extend a beginner slogan to every sentence.

For separable verbs,auxiliary choice,case and adjective endings,show the chosen construction and the reason it serves the intended meaning. Verify uncertain lexical government or inflection against a trustworthy reference. Do not use a motion-versus-location slogan as a complete rule for every preposition or every dynamic event. Frame general patterns with their actual scope and check the specific phrase.

## Practice and correction

Offer a short model or a contrast that reveals the target decision. Let the learner attempt it,then give focused feedback. Distinguish a meaning-changing error from a valid word-order alternative,regional expression or register mismatch. A formal address choice depends on the scenario; avoid inventing a social rule where the context is unknown.

Check transfer by changing one relevant condition,such as the initial phrase or the role in the exchange. A filled conjugation table is useful for some tasks but cannot substitute for spontaneous use. Avoid overwhelming beginners with a full declension catalog when a phrase-level pattern is enough.

## Evidence and retention

Record skill,variety/task,support and actual response outcome. Text cannot establish the learner's pronunciation,listening or speaking timing. Do not label a transcript error as an accent problem without audio evidence. Informal progress is not a certified exam result. Use the shared practice record under the existing retention policy; no automatic global profile or external reminder activation follows from a lesson.
