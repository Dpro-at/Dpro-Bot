# Language coaching behavioral briefs

These are future tutoring trials, not proof of actual learner improvement.

## Arabic variety and task

A learner requests a daily spoken interaction in a named dialect but types a mixture of formal and regional forms. Acceptance: preserve the target variety, distinguish valid variation from the selected learning error, give manageable support and invite a fresh attempt. No automatic formal-standard correction of every line or claimed pronunciation assessment from text.

## Conversation flow

A learner wants fluency practice for a workplace scenario and delayed correction. Acceptance: meaningful in-role turns, learner space, clarification when needed and a focused debrief with observed evidence. Do not answer both roles or interrupt every turn with a grammar table.

## Reading and evidence

A learner reads a short supplied passage and answers with help. Acceptance: check understanding against the passage, separate hint-assisted from independent performance and vary the follow-up. Do not assign a certified global level from that task or persist private answers automatically.

For any actual trial, record the request, modality, support, outcome and uncertainty under the permitted data policy. Discovery/parsing cannot establish pedagogical quality.


## Language-specific lesson transfer

English:a learner requests a current-relevance versus finished-time contrast in a chosen variety. Acceptance:preserve intended meaning,use a verified contextual contrast,accept valid alternatives and check a fresh use rather than keyword matching.

French:a learner writes a noun group with a meaningful agreement issue and a possible typing slip. Acceptance:inspect the phrase,correct the selected task target proportionally and distinguish spoken/written evidence. Do not ignore every accent or infer pronunciation from text.

German:a learner places a multiword phrase first in an ordinary declarative clause. Acceptance:explain constituent versus word position with a suitable verified example,then check a new sentence. Do not extend the main-clause pattern to every construction.

Other language:a learner requests a variety with no dedicated guide. Acceptance:use the adaptation route,reliable references and explicit confidence limits;do not substitute another language or invent proficiency certification.


## Spanish variety and meaning

Given a learner-selected regional context and a valid local address form,teach the requested task without replacing the variety. Acceptance:contextual verified explanation,focused feedback,fresh transfer and no pronunciation claim from text or accredited rating from a short chat.
