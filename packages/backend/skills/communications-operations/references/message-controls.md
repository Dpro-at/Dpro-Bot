# Message and release controls

Bind a communication to audience, objective, source facts, confidentiality class, decision owner and exact revision. A fact approved for one audience may remain restricted for another. Keep confirmed facts, proposed interpretation and unresolved questions separate; do not disguise a gap with polished wording.

Map claims and quotations to inspected evidence and approval state. Use synthetic or minimized examples in reusable material. A manager briefing is not a private employee case record. Route material legal, policy or incident uncertainty to the actual responsible reviewer without inventing a deadline, liability admission or entitlement.

A draft, approved revision, queued delivery, provider acceptance and verified audience receipt are distinct. Changes to claims, recipients or attachments invalidate affected review assumptions. No send, upload, public announcement or staff notification is implied by preparation. For a separately authorized uncertain submission, retain its identity and reconcile state before retrying. Measure reach, understanding and action separately; absence of feedback is not proof of comprehension.
