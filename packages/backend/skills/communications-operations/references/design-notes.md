# Design notes

Original public-relations and internal-change preparation modes share claims, audience confidentiality, versioned review and delivery-state controls. They preserve separate spokesperson/policy ownership and do not supply messaging accounts or public authority. No external skill text or licensed adaptation was copied. Blank templates contain no private records. Normal, missing-capability and adversarial task briefs are future evaluation inputs; import/format checks and actual model, audience and live-action acceptance remain separate.
