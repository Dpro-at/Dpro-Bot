# Communications operations

Prepare a message that its intended audience can use without overstating facts, approval or authority. Establish purpose, source evidence, audience and exact decision/release state. Read [message controls](references/message-controls.md) for claims, confidentiality, revisions and delivery evidence.

Select [public relations](references/public-relations.md) for media briefs, public statements and Q&A; select [internal change](references/internal-change.md) for employee/change messages, manager briefs and feedback plans. Use the [media response](assets/media-response.md) or [change message](assets/change-message.md) template. These modes share factual controls but have different audiences and decision owners; do not broadcast an internal case as public evidence.

Deliver complete copy and the precise unresolved source/decision list. Preparing a statement does not appoint a spokesperson or approve its release. No contact, employee send, publication, subscription or account access is supplied. Read [runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md) and [acceptance](references/acceptance.md) to separate portable preparation from actual model, audience and delivery acceptance.

[Design notes](references/design-notes.md) record original authorship, comparison boundaries and unobserved acceptance.
