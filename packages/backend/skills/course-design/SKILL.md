# Course design

Create instruction that enables the intended learner to do something observable. Begin with the performance need, audience and delivery context. A syllabus of topic headings is not a complete course, and a quiz score cannot demonstrate every practical skill.

Read [Runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md) in a new host. Keep maintained files in English. Inspect any supplied curriculum, course materials, learning platform constraints and institutional requirements before rebuilding. A request to repair a few gaps does not authorize replacing the whole course or publishing to a learning platform.

## Define the task and learner

Use [Course brief](assets/course-brief.md). Identify what learners need to do, what they can already do, the relevant constraints and why instruction is an appropriate response. A missing permission, broken process or inaccessible tool is not necessarily a training gap. Distinguish verified learner evidence from designer assumptions.

Read [Outcomes and scope](references/outcomes-scope.md) to make objectives observable. Reuse useful existing content and terminology. Select an attainable scope for the actual time/modality rather than filling a fixed number of modules. Unknown institutional policies, dates, contacts and grading rules remain explicit gaps; do not invent authoritative defaults.

## Align practice and evidence

Read [Activities and assessment](references/activities-assessment.md). Map intended outcomes to meaningful practice and observable assessment evidence. Inspect the actual task demand rather than classifying it from a verb or format alone. A multiple-choice case can test judgment, while a large project can still amount to copied procedure.

Use [Alignment map](assets/alignment-map.md) and [Assessment specification](assets/assessment-specification.md). Optional [Structural audit](references/structural-audit.md) checks a declared ID/reference manifest for missing links and unknown references. It cannot establish cognitive alignment, validity, fairness or learning quality.

## Author usable materials

Read [Module authoring](references/module-authoring.md). Produce the scope requested: complete learner instructions, relevant explanations/examples, practice, feedback/answers where appropriate, assessment/rubric and facilitator needs. Do not call a collection of placeholders or topics a delivered lesson. Show material gaps and source/rights dependencies honestly.

Adapt support to the audience and observed difficulty. Offer foundations or alternative paths where a mixed audience needs them; avoid forcing all learners through one assumed prerequisite ladder. Maintain clear links between materials and the authoritative objective/assessment records.

## Review and pilot

Read [Delivery and evaluation](references/delivery-evaluation.md). Review content accuracy, instructions, accessibility, feasibility and assessment evidence. A structural pass is preliminary. A representative learner/facilitator pilot can expose hidden prerequisites and unrealistic timing, but requires actual authorized participants and records; do not invent a pilot or its results.

Use [Review and pilot receipt](assets/review-pilot.md) to distinguish authored, reviewed, locally packaged, platform-previewed and learner-tested states. LMS publishing, enrollment, learner-data processing and external messaging retain their own permissions. [Acceptance briefs](references/acceptance.md) are future tasks. [Design notes](references/design-notes.md) record upstream coverage and tradeoffs.

## Practical professional training

For a workplace skill workshop,coached demonstration or transfer plan,read [Professional practice](references/professional-practice.md). Reuse the outcomes,materials and assessment workflow above,with observed task criteria and authorized learner evidence rather than another duplicate course package. Use [Training session](assets/training-session.md) for delivery and follow-up.

For a staff learning program across roles/cohorts,read [Staff training](references/staff-training.md) and use [Training program](assets/training-program.md). This extends practical training with actual role coverage,delivery ownership and private progress boundaries.
