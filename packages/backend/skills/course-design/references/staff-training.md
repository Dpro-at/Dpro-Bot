# Staff training programs

Start with the organization's actual task needs,role groups,current procedure and supplied evidence. Distinguish onboarding learning,skill development,mandatory policy training and a specific operational change. Do not label a course mandatory or certify compliance without the real policy/authority.

## Program and coverage

Define relevant roles/cohorts and the observable work each needs to perform. Use existing course materials and practical-training resources. Identify common foundations and role-specific tasks without duplicating whole courses unnecessarily. A job title alone does not establish current competence or access.

Map each required outcome to usable instruction,practice,feedback and evidence. Record actual prerequisites,tools,delivery capacity,facilitator ownership and realistic time. Include access/accommodation routes under the organization's process. Missing equipment,permission or scheduling capacity is a blocker to resolve,not a reason to label an employee unmotivated.

## Delivery plan

Prepare the program,cohort schedule and facilitator/task resources within the requested scope. Actual enrollment,calendar invites,messages,LMS changes and purchases require their applicable authorization. A planned session or sent link is not proof that training occurred or was completed.

Use the organization's actual requirement,deadline,renewal and completion definitions. Do not create a universal annual cadence or arbitrary pass threshold. Where the policy is missing,record the owner decision needed and complete independent content work.

## Evidence and privacy

Separate assigned,enrolled,attended,completed,assessed and demonstrated-on-the-job states. Attendance or self-confidence cannot alone establish competence. Record assistance and conditions for practical assessments,and use a fresh relevant task when checking transfer.

Keep individual progress and assessment records limited to the authorized purpose/audience. Aggregate reports can still expose small groups or sensitive details. Do not export employee histories with reusable settings/templates or convert sparse training observations into employment decisions.

## Improve and hand off

Use actual learner/facilitator evidence to revise the relevant instruction,practice or support. Keep domain review,program approval,delivery and workplace outcomes separate. Report what is prepared versus actually observed,remaining coverage/access gaps and the next responsible owner action. No real participant,learning or compliance result is invented by the planning workflow.
