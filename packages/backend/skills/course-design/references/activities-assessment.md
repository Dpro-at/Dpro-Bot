# Activities and assessment

For each required outcome, identify how learners will practice the actual operation and what evidence can show it. A complete map names the content/input, learner action, support, feedback and assessment. A link in a table does not prove the activity prepares the learner.

## Practice

Use a meaningful task with clear inputs, output and completion criterion. A learner should know what to do without guessing the instructor's intent. Include enough relevant information and tools, then choose scaffolding appropriate to the starting point. A reading can provide necessary input, but a required decision or procedure also needs an opportunity to perform or reason through it.

Vary context when transfer matters. Repeating a demonstrated answer can check attention or recall; it does not alone demonstrate independent application. Provide feedback at a useful point and allow a relevant correction/retry when the objective requires practice. Avoid decorative interactions that consume time without supporting the outcome.

## Assessment demand

Choose evidence from the intended skill, conditions and stakes. A short selected-response case may assess interpretation if the distractors distinguish reasoning. A practical task may require an artifact, trace, explanation or observed process. Do not infer quality solely from the assessment's format or size.

Specify the task, permitted resources, submission, evaluation criteria and feedback. Use observable criterion descriptions rather than excellent/good/poor labels without meaning. Separate content correctness from presentation polish unless presentation is part of the outcome. Make weightings and pass rules explicit when supplied; do not invent institutional grading policy.

Include expected answers or scoring guidance appropriate to the task and protect them from accidental inclusion in the learner-facing version when needed. Accept multiple valid solutions where the domain permits them. Check that question wording and irrelevant language difficulty do not obscure the intended skill.

## Alignment review

Inspect the actual objective, activity instructions, assessment and rubric together. Identify missing practice, missing evidence, mismatched conditions or a rubric that rewards a different skill. Record the reason and a concrete correction. The optional structural helper detects only declared reference/coverage gaps; human/domain review must assess meaning.

Administrative or enrichment activities can be intentionally outside assessed outcomes. Keep them separate from the helper's declared instructional manifest or explain the exception in the course record. Do not force a meaningless objective onto every welcome message to satisfy a machine check.

## Learner evidence

A completion click, satisfaction survey and assessment performance answer different questions. Keep participation, enjoyment, immediate achievement and later task transfer separate. Do not claim training caused a workplace outcome from an uncontrolled before/after observation without discussing other explanations.
