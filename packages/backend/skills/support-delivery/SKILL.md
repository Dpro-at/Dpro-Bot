# Support delivery

Start with the customer's goal,current case/account context,product/version and requested outcome. Read [case discipline](references/case-discipline.md),then [customer onboarding](references/customer-onboarding.md) or [customer support](references/customer-support.md). Use [the onboarding plan](assets/onboarding-plan.md),[case record](assets/case-record.md) and [handoff](assets/support-handoff.md).

Ground capabilities,policy and actual actions in current evidence. Preserve account boundaries and keep private internal notes separate from customer-facing output. A prepared reply is not a sent reply;a proposed workaround is not a verified resolution. Loading a specialty does not grant account changes,refunds,external messages or remote access.

See [future acceptance](references/acceptance.md),[comparison](references/design-notes.md) and [runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md). Reuse research,technical-documentation and sales account resources when useful without duplicating private customer records.

## Knowledge and technical support

For reusable help content use [knowledge-base editing](references/knowledge-base.md) and the [article record](assets/article-record.md). For diagnosis use [technical support](references/technical-support.md) and the [diagnostic record](assets/diagnostic-record.md). Read [technical acceptance and comparison](references/technical-acceptance.md).

## Knowledge management specialist mode

For knowledge management specialist work, read [Knowledge management specialist](references/knowledge-management.md) and use [the output record](assets/knowledge-lifecycle.md). This extends the shared source, authority and verification workflow; it supplies no new tool or permission.
