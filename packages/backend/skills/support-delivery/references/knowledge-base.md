# Knowledge-base editing

## Establish the reader and source

Identify the actual question/task,product/version,audience,permissions and supported environment. Read the complete relevant resolution or authoritative documentation;an old ticket workaround may no longer apply. Search the existing knowledge base before adding another article. Update a suitable canonical article when the purpose is the same,and record meaningful changes instead of creating competing answers.

Distinguish how-to,troubleshooting,reference,FAQ and known-issue content by reader need. A troubleshooting article can state an unknown cause while giving verified diagnostic steps;do not invent a root cause to fill a heading. Remove private customer details,internal-only links,secrets and unapproved product claims from public-facing content.

## Write actionable material

Use the customer's terms and actual error wording where safe. Explain applicability,prerequisites and expected outcome before the steps. Sequence each action with enough UI/command context to perform it,and include what success or failure looks like. Preserve literal values and syntax;do not silently replace a real parameter with a plausible example without labeling it.

Separate reversible checks,workarounds and consequential changes. State material side effects,required authority and recovery options. Avoid recommending deletion,resets or security-control disabling as a routine shortcut. A generic command must be adapted to the actual platform and tested scope before publication.

## Verify and maintain

Walk through the steps in an authorized representative environment where available. Record product/version,actual result and unavailable checks. A source edit or link check does not prove the rendered article is usable or that the procedure works. Check heading structure,readability,accessibility-relevant text,links and consistency with current UI.

Assign an owner and review trigger based on product change,risk and user feedback rather than an arbitrary universal calendar. For known issues maintain actual affected versions,status and workaround limits;do not retain a promised fix date after it becomes stale. Track search failures and recurring cases as evidence for improvements,without claiming article traffic alone proves resolution.

## Deliver

Provide the article,source/verification record,metadata and outstanding reviewer questions. Keep internal diagnostic detail separate from public instructions. Publication,archival,deletion and customer notification follow actual authority. Reuse technical-documentation and editorial resources where available. No article draft is automatically published or proof that customers can self-resolve.
