# Customer onboarding

## Define successful first use

Identify customer objectives,user roles,current setup,product scope and desired first useful outcome. Check existing onboarding records and commitments before creating another plan. A completed signup or imported dataset is not necessarily customer value. Agree evidence that the intended workflow works and identify who can accept it.

Map prerequisites:account ownership,permissions,environment compatibility,data readiness,integration credentials and relevant policy. Avoid requesting secrets in chat or granting broad access for convenience. Distinguish prerequisites controlled by the customer,provider and another team. Record dependencies instead of promising a date that assumes all are already met.

## Build a practical path

Sequence a minimal useful workflow,then optional advanced configuration. Give each milestone a purpose,owner,prerequisite,expected output and validation. Tailor learning to actual roles and accessibility needs. Reuse approved guides and examples with synthetic data;do not expose another customer's configuration as a tutorial.

For imports or integrations,plan mapping,test records,validation,error handling and rollback before a bulk operation. Preview counts and consequential changes under the actual authorization. A successful connector login does not prove data synchronization or correct permissions. Check the intended end-to-end use with representative data where permitted.

## Support adoption and handoff

Track blockers,questions and agreed actions in a shared plan. Distinguish product gaps,configuration issues and training needs. Provide concise guidance and confirm understanding through a relevant task rather than assuming a meeting attendance proves readiness. Avoid unnecessary upsell during a blocked setup.

At handoff,record configured scope,verified outcomes,known limits,admin ownership and support path. Define a follow-up point with the responsible person. Account provisioning,migration,scheduling and outbound communication require actual authority. Deliver the plan and actual verification receipt;do not claim activation,adoption or retention from a checklist alone.
