# Customer support

## Triage the real issue

Read the available case history and identify expected versus actual behavior,start time,affected workflow/users,environment and workaround. Ask the smallest useful missing question. Treat customer interpretation as a hypothesis when the cause is unknown. Search authorized known issues and related tickets before creating duplicate work,but do not merge cases merely because their symptoms sound similar.

Recommend category,priority and owner using the organization's process and actual impact. Security exposure,data loss and billing disputes may need specialized handling even when the UI remains usable. Keep suspected incident escalation separate from confirmed root cause. State search coverage and unresolved identity instead of inventing account facts.

## Resolve with safe guidance

Use current product behavior and approved policy. Provide clear steps with prerequisites,expected result and what to report if the step fails. Prefer a narrow reversible check before a broad reset. Do not ask the customer to disable security controls,delete data or run an unreviewed script as a generic troubleshooting shortcut.

Distinguish a documented workaround,an untested suggestion and a confirmed fix. If the answer requires engineering,billing or security authority,prepare a useful escalation with evidence,impact,steps tried and a specific decision needed. Avoid transferring the customer without context or promising another team's deadline.

## Write and follow through

Prepare a reply matched to the channel,customer knowledge and actual state. Answer directly,explain material limits and give one clear next action or update commitment that can be met. Do not expose internal notes,unapproved roadmap information or another customer's data. A request for a feature is feedback,not an approved promise to build it.

After an authorized action verify the relevant state and report actual outcome. Track unresolved dependencies and the next owner. Case closure should reflect confirmed resolution,an agreed workaround or the actual policy for inactive cases,with status stated honestly. Deliver a reply draft,case record and escalation where needed;actual sending,routing,refunds or account changes follow separate authority.
