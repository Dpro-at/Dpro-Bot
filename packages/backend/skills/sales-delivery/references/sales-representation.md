# Sales conversations and follow-through

## Prepare a relevant conversation

Confirm prospect/account identity, existing owner, relationship stage, relevant history and the requested outcome. Read available prior exchanges with sufficient context to avoid repeating an answered question or treating an existing customer as a cold lead. Use verified product capabilities, supported proof and the actual commercial position. When evidence is missing, prepare a question rather than inventing a personalized hook.

Prepare a small number of discovery questions about the customer's current process, desired outcome, constraints, impact and decision process. Let answers guide follow-up. Do not turn a qualification framework into an interrogation or equate an objection with permission to pressure someone. Separate the customer's words from your interpretation in notes.

## Communicate value honestly

Connect a supported capability to the stated problem and explain the relevant limitation or prerequisite. Use an approved case study with its actual scope; another customer's results do not guarantee this customer's outcome. Estimate ROI only with visible assumptions and relevant baselines. Never invent product features, integrations, customer references, urgency, scarcity or competitor claims.

For objections, identify whether the issue concerns suitability, evidence, risk, timing, price or authority. Acknowledge the specific concern, answer with relevant support, and propose a proportionate next step. If the product does not fit, say so. Pricing, discounts, legal terms and delivery promises stay within actual authority; unresolved terms go to the responsible person.

## Prepare outreach and follow-up

Use the requested voice, recipient and channel. Ground personalization in relevant business evidence and provide one clear next step. A warm reply should preserve thread context and recipient identity. Sequence length, interval and copy length depend on the situation; do not impose a universal three-touch pattern or arbitrary word limit. Each follow-up needs a reason beyond escalating pressure.

Before real outreach check applicable channel/jurisdiction rules, suppression status and sender identity through the authorized workflow. Do not assume business-to-business messages are universally exempt from commercial-email rules. Prepare drafts or a sequence plan when asked; actual sending, enrollment or scheduling requires actual user authority and host permissions. Respect opt-outs and avoid sensitive personal speculation.

## Close the loop

Record agreed next steps with owner, date and dependency. Distinguish a suggested meeting from a booked meeting, an interest signal from an agreement, and verbal intent from a signed contract. Provide a faithful call summary, open questions, proposed follow-up and CRM change list as requested. Do not move an opportunity to won or create a customer commitment from an enthusiastic comment. Verify authorized actions and report uncertain outcomes without retrying blindly.
