# Account management

## Build the account picture

Identify the account entity and parent/subsidiary relationships, assigned owner, plan horizon and purpose: onboarding, ongoing value, renewal, recovery or expansion. Check whether a current plan exists and refresh it rather than producing competing versions. Establish actual products, contract period, service commitments and available adoption/support context. Do not treat a CRM deal close date as the contractual renewal date without evidence.

Separate customer-stated outcomes, observed use, account-team interpretation and proposed opportunities. Map each meaningful objective to evidence, current progress and a way to assess success. A high usage count may coexist with an unresolved business problem; a quiet contact may simply be on leave. Avoid assigning a health color from one signal without the organization's definition and sample coverage.

## Map relationships and delivery risk

Record known stakeholders by business role, decision involvement, relationship owner and evidence. A title alone does not establish budget authority or sponsorship. Identify gaps in technical, commercial, procurement and executive coverage where relevant; do not invent missing people. Coordinate through existing ownership and avoid parallel approaches that undermine the account relationship.

Review open commitments, incidents and customer concerns with dates, owners and current state. Distinguish delivery risk, adoption friction, commercial exposure and relationship continuity. An unresolved support issue is not automatically a churn prediction. Propose actions matched to actual evidence: clarify success criteria, arrange a relevant review, resolve a commitment or validate a renewal dependency.

## Plan value and renewal

Build an account plan with customer objectives, current evidence, stakeholder coverage, commitments, risks and dated next actions. For a business review, show achieved outcomes and unresolved gaps with defensible measures; avoid vanity metrics or invented ROI. Where a financial estimate is useful, show assumptions and separate estimated benefit from measured customer value.

For renewals, verify contract dates, notice dependencies, scope and authorized commercial position. Never promise a discount, remedy, delivery date or amendment beyond actual authority. Expansion is a hypothesis until need, suitability and buying process are established; do not turn every support conversation into an upsell.

## Maintain continuity

Assign a real accountable owner and next review point for each proposed action. Track changes since the previous version and preserve resolved context instead of rewriting history. Deliver an account plan and a concise customer-facing agenda or internal handoff as requested. Customer communications and CRM synchronization are separate authorized steps under the operating contract. Account-plan quality does not prove customer acceptance, retention or revenue.
