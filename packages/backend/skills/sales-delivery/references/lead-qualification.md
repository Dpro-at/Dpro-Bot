# Lead qualification

## Identify and contextualize

Resolve the lead/contact/account with stable identifiers and match uncertainty. Check existing ownership, open opportunities, recent relevant contact and suppression or do-not-contact status when available. Similar company names or shared email domains are candidate matches, not sufficient authority to merge records. Coordinate with an existing account owner instead of assigning the lead to yourself.

Understand the actual target customer definition and permitted disqualifiers for this motion. Consumer, nonprofit, enterprise and partner leads need different dimensions. Mark irrelevant dimensions as not applicable and unavailable dimensions as unknown. Missing budget information is not evidence that no budget exists. Do not create arbitrary company-size or geographic cutoffs.

## Assess fit and intent separately

For fit, evaluate relevant use case, service/product suitability, organization constraints and buyer role using cited evidence. For intent, distinguish an explicit evaluation request, a question, a content download, historical engagement and inferred public signals. A recent funding round or job posting can suggest a topic to investigate; it does not establish willingness to buy.

Use the organization's qualification method when supplied, but preserve the underlying evidence rather than filling framework boxes with guesses. State which conclusions depend on missing information. If numerical scoring is requested, define scale, weights, treatment of unknowns and tie-breaking. Do not force a fixed percentage of leads into the highest priority or disguise an uncalibrated heuristic as a predictive probability.

## Recommend the next action

Provide a reasoned priority and relevant next question or action. Apply a real response policy where known; otherwise label the proposed service target as a proposal. Keep priority, ownership, qualification state and contact permission distinct. A strong-fit lead who opted out remains suppressed from outreach.

For a backlog, include source row/record, fit, intent, unknowns, owner, priority rationale and suggested next step for every reviewed item. Explain ties and exclusions. Do not claim the whole backlog is reviewed if only a sample or first page was available. Route unresolved identity and contradictory evidence for review rather than dropping difficult rows.

## Handoff responsibly

Prepare a concise handoff with need, source, current ownership, evidence, unanswered questions and recommended action. Sending it or changing qualification/owner fields follows actual user authority and the operating contract. Qualification is not a sale, and a useful ranking is not proof of measured conversion performance. Keep actual response and downstream outcomes available for later calibration without leaking customer data into the portable package.
