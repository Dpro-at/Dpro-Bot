# Research brief

Use only the sections needed by the question. Replace instructions with findings; remove empty sections.

## Answer
State the supported conclusion and the decision it helps.

## Scope
Question, constraints, assumptions and as-of date when relevant.

## Findings
Use a short narrative or comparison table. Put direct source links beside consequential claims. Distinguish facts from inference.

## Uncertainty
Conflicting evidence, missing access, non-comparable data or conditions that could change the conclusion.

## Next step
One practical next check or action when the user needs it. A recommendation is not permission to execute.
