# Design notes and comparison

Complete selected entrypoints reviewed:

- [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/blob/33d3dea41a341b341d4f5da2e8801ae0c89be2b8/legal/skills/review-contract/SKILL.md),commit `33d3dea41a341b341d4f5da2e8801ae0c89be2b8`.
- [LegalQuants/lq-ai](https://github.com/LegalQuants/lq-ai/blob/671eab0da79e48d5080ed0039ee217ecd0e00337/skills/case-law-research/SKILL.md),commit `671eab0da79e48d5080ed0039ee217ecd0e00337`.

Captures/hashes are private;linked connector/reference trees were not exhaustively reviewed. Original prompts,clause guidance,examples,language and templates are not redistributed.

The contract source offers broad playbook review and proposed redlines. Our independently authored package retains useful document/issue/drafting coverage with explicit operative-document,precedence,version and cross-clause evidence. It does not copy universal clause ranges,traffic-light positions or legal deadlines. Without a playbook it performs labeled objective-based issue spotting rather than inventing market standards.

The case-law source offers a precise US CourtListener gateway workflow and strong text grounding. Our provider-neutral research mode supports actual jurisdiction/source selection,legislation/regulations as relevant and transparent history verification. It does not claim connector availability,global corpus coverage or citator acceptance. Less turnkey gateway integration is the tradeoff. No measured model-task or legal-quality superiority is claimed.

The [US Supreme Court opinion publication overview](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/opinions.aspx) was checked as a narrow example of opinion types and evolving publication versions. It informs source/version distinctions,not a universal court hierarchy or current-good-law conclusion. No court text is copied.

No generic citation checker was added:matching a citation pattern cannot prove existence,holding or current treatment. Native parsing/discovery and isolated library trials are preparation evidence only;actual legal task,source-currentness,document editing and responsible-review acceptance remain separate.
