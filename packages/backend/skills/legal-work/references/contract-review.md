# Contract review and negotiation preparation

## Read the operative arrangement

Read the relevant complete document set before final conclusions. Track definitions,precedence,cross-references,exceptions and amendments. A limitation clause may interact with indemnity,remedy,insurance and survival provisions. Do not label a term unacceptable solely because its heading is missing;the substance may be elsewhere.

Map the actual obligations and allocation:scope/deliverables,acceptance,fees/tax/changes,term/renewal/notices,termination/transition,confidentiality,data/security,IP/license,liability/remedies,indemnity,insurance,assignment and dispute terms as relevant. Avoid filling a universal checklist with irrelevant findings. Identify who must do what,when,under which trigger,and how the parties can verify it.

## Evidence and impact

For each material issue record exact section/version,the contract's actual effect,playbook/objective basis,business consequence and remaining legal uncertainty. Separate a drafting ambiguity from a clear commercial choice or an unverified legal restriction. Do not invent typical caps,notice periods,mandatory mutuality or statutory deadlines. If law matters,research the actual jurisdiction and current source rather than importing a generic table.

Prioritize through the actual deal,exposure,dependencies and owner policy. A risk label is not a substitute for explaining a concrete consequence. Preserve acceptable terms and avoid unnecessary rewriting. Missing incorporated documents remain an evidence gap;do not claim they contain unfavorable terms without seeing them.

## Proposed edits

Provide exact replacement/insertion text when requested,with its target,rationale and tradeoff. Align definitions,numbering,cross-references and related clauses. Clearly mark proposals versus accepted wording. Offer a fallback only when it follows the actual negotiation authority;do not make concessions or call a clause non-negotiable on the user's behalf.

Check the whole arrangement after the change. A broader indemnity may undermine the liability structure;a termination edit can affect prepaid fees,data export and survival. Keep drafting concise enough to insert without adding unsupported promises. Preserve the original and the actual edit history.

## Deliverable

Give the scoped review basis,material issues,proposed language,missing documents and precise reviewer decisions. A contract summary is not a completed redlined file. Sending to the counterparty,signature,CLM routing or accepting risk requires the applicable authority and actual result evidence.
