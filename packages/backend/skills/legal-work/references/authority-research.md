# Jurisdiction-aware authority research

## Search plan

Frame each issue with the relevant jurisdiction,forum,date and material facts. Determine whether the question needs legislation,regulations,judgments,agency materials,treaty/contract sources or a combination. Use secondary commentary to discover terms and sources,then verify decisive propositions in the actual primary text. Keep official versions and publication status visible.

Search specific issues and synonyms,record queries/filters and broaden deliberately when coverage is thin. Do not fabricate a case,section or quotation from memory to complete a report. Search results and headnotes are leads,not proof of a holding. A connector's empty result means nothing was found in that searched corpus/window,not that no authority exists.

## Read and classify

Before attributing a rule,read the supporting passage and enough context to understand conditions,exceptions,procedural posture and outcome. For cases distinguish majority/plurality,concurrence,dissent,order and summary material. Record court,date,source document/version,pinpoint and whether the proposition is a holding or another observation. Binding/persuasive status depends on the actual system and forum;do not infer it from prominence alone.

For legislation/regulations check effective dates,amendments,definitions,territorial scope,exceptions and any relevant transitional provisions. A future provision or draft is not automatically current law. Record unresolved currency or translation issues rather than silently relying on a convenient older text.

## History and conflicts

Subsequent treatment/current status is a separate verification step. If a citator or equivalent authoritative history review was unavailable,say so. A retrieved judgment proves its text,not that it remains good law. Distinguish conflicting jurisdictions,factual settings and later developments without forcing a universal rule.

## Synthesis

Organize by issue:question,verified source/proposition,relevant facts,reasoned connection,counterauthority and uncertainty. Separate descriptive research from proposed legal application or a reviewer decision. Supply traceable links/citations and pinpoints with faithful limited quotations where useful. Keep coverage gaps,queries and next verification actions in the research record. Do not claim exhaustive research or formal reliance from one corpus or a plausible citation format.
