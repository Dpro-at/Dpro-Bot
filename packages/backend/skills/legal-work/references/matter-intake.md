# Matter and document intake

Identify the requested outcome,entity/party perspective,transaction or issue,material facts,jurisdiction,forum and relevant date. Distinguish governing law,venue and the places where conduct/data/parties are located;one clause does not settle every applicable rule. Record the responsible business/legal reviewer and actual action scope. Do not assume a US jurisdiction or court hierarchy for a question that did not specify one.

For agreements identify the current version,parties,execution status,main terms,schedules,incorporated policies,order forms and amendments. Confirm available text is readable and complete enough for the requested review. OCR errors,missing pages or unavailable linked terms can change meaning;mark their location and effect. Do not silently treat a scanned summary as the operative agreement.

Find the actual approved playbook or prior instruction,including its version,party role,acceptable ranges and escalation ownership. If none exists,perform a clearly labeled issue-spotting review against the user's objectives rather than inventing market standards or organization policy. Do not delay every task to manufacture a playbook.

For research turn the question into specific issues and material factual assumptions. Identify the requested depth,deadline and source access. Separate a roadmap from completed research. Use accessible current authoritative sources where possible;paid database availability or a connector name does not establish permission to purchase,query privileged matter data or certify current-law status.

Keep private matter identifiers and record references in approved locations. Reusable templates need no actual client names,contract text,personal data or credentials. Record unresolved inputs and the useful work that can proceed without them.
