# Analysis report

## Answer
Finding, population, period and the decision it informs.

## Evidence
Small useful table or inspected figure with labels and denominators.

## Method
Source/snapshot, query or transformation, exclusions and definitions sufficient to reproduce the result.

## Validation
Counts, nulls, key/join checks, reconciliation and independent calculation actually performed.

## Limits and next step
Material uncertainty, scope of inference and the next useful check. Do not imply a causal conclusion or operational action beyond the evidence and authorization.
