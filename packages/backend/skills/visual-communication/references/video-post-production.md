# Video editing and finishing

## Source and edit intent

Identify objective,audience,source files/versions,rights,requested changes,destination and actual tooling. Inspect the real media properties:duration,streams,frame/timebase characteristics,resolution/orientation,color information,audio tracks and available caption/transcript sources. Do not guess camera behavior from a filename. Keep originals and write a new version unless actual replacement is authorized.

Build an edit decision from content,not just a loudness or scene-change proxy. Record source in/out,sequence position,purpose,transition,audio and caption dependencies. A highlight candidate score cannot decide what matters. Distinguish a faithful factual edit from a fictional reconstruction or illustrative montage;do not splice statements into misleading claims.

## Timing and processing

Keep source time,timeline time,frame indexing and the chosen timebase explicit. Frame-exact cuts differ from keyframe/stream-copy boundaries. Variable frame rate,retiming,multicam offsets and long-recording drift need actual inspection;do not treat one successful initial sync as proof of whole-program lip sync.

Use a minimal supported processing path and preserve quality proportionately. Lossless/remux options can suit some requests but cannot guarantee arbitrary frame-exact results. Avoid repeated lossy encodes where a suitable timeline/master path exists. Proxies are working artifacts,not final quality acceptance.

Review orientation,crop/pad/resize choices against the actual subject and message. Alternate aspect ratios may need recomposition;do not remove important context silently. HDR/Log/SDR transforms and metadata retagging are different operations. Check actual color intent/tool support instead of forcing an SDR preset or claiming a LUT fixes unknown footage.

## Captions,audio and review

Ground captions in actual speech/text and review timing,speaker labels,needed non-speech information,line breaks,readability and correct font shaping. Automatic transcription is a starting point,not verified accessibility. Recheck captions after retiming/crops and preserve intended meaning when text edits are authorized. Choose burned-in versus selectable tracks for the real delivery requirement.

Inspect actual frames at changed regions and playback across cuts,transitions,sync and audio. Probe/metadata results cannot see an obstructed face or hear an awkward join. Contact sheets do not establish smooth motion or full lip-sync. Record actual inspected frames/listening scope and remaining unavailable checks.

## Export and handoff

Use current destination criteria for codec/container,pixel/color format,frame rate,channels,loudness,captions and access. Verify actual output,decode/playback,duration/streams and relevant visual/audio observations. A completed encoder or file-exists check is only part of acceptance.

Provide editable timeline/project,media dependencies/rights,actual reviewed exports and a concrete receipt where in scope. If tools are unavailable,deliver a full edit packet and missing prerequisite without installing or pretending to render. External uploads,paid transcription/generation,private media transfer and publication require actual authority. Reconcile uncertain submissions before retry.
