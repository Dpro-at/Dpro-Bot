# Illustration craft and visual grammar

## Subject and action

State what the illustration must explain or evoke, the actual subject/action and where it will be viewed. A decorative spot, explanatory diagram and editorial scene need different detail and factual precision. Sketch the focal relationship before choosing surface effects.

## Coherent construction

Define a small useful grammar: shape family, proportion, perspective, line/fill behavior, palette roles, lighting/shading and detail density. Apply it consistently while allowing the subject to determine meaningful variation. Do not treat a named style as a license to copy another artist's distinctive assets or measured parts kit.

Check silhouette, pose, weight/contact, occlusion and relevant anatomy or structural mechanics. A hand, prop or connection point can carry the main action; inspect it instead of hiding an ambiguity behind decorative detail. For explanatory content, preserve actual relationships rather than drawing an attractive but misleading diagram.

## Composition and scale

Choose a focal point and a readable relationship between subject, supporting forms and background. Inspect at actual display size and against intended neighboring content. Small details can turn into noise or disappear. Avoid introducing an unrelated palette or brand identity merely because a reference used it.

## Vector and raster production

For vector assets, inspect path organization, bounds/viewBox, clipping, strokes, text dependencies and export scale. A valid SVG can still crop the subject or contain unsupported filters in the target renderer. For raster work, inspect resolution, edges, transparency and compression at the intended size. A preview embedded in a design tool is not the exported file.

## Originality and rights

Use references for principles and task constraints. Keep source/rights notes for imported material and distinguish generated, drawn, licensed and user-supplied assets where relevant. Do not claim exclusive ownership or clearance without evidence. Deliver editable source and required exports, with actual visual inspection or explicit unverified status.
