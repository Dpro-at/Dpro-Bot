# Activity coaching design and comparison

Three pinned selected upstream entrypoints were reviewed: running-coach-skill,fitness-copilot and life-coach. Private batch24 captures retain commits and hashes. They provide useful history-aware planning,session detail and user-led coaching patterns. Their linked reference trees and scripts were not exhaustively audited or installed.

This independently authored package supplies full activity/habit,fitness and mobility workflows with editable records. It avoids mandatory wearable/MCP dependencies,automatic profile persistence,medical/credential role claims and hard-coded pain clearance or automatic injury resolution. It does not copy scripts,templates,exercise catalogs or branded integrations. No universal weekly progression rule or mandatory personal-record target is included.

Tradeoffs: no wearable sync,HTML workout renderer,automatic planner,clinical protocol or extensive sport-specific exercise library. Upstream automation breadth is greater in those areas. This package prioritizes provider-neutral,nonclinical operation with explicit evidence and tool boundaries. Broader boundary coverage does not prove better coaching or safer real exercise. No matched model-task or physical-outcome comparison has run.

[WHO physical activity guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity) supports general population context and gradual accessible activity;individual suitability and dosing require actual context. A requested NHS flexibility page returned404 and is not claimed reviewed. Real consequential health guidance needs current primary sources. No personal health data,exercise execution,device upload,paid service or autonomous contact is included.
