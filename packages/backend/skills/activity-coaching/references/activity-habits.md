# Activity and habits coaching

## Choose the person's outcome

Ask what change would be useful in the person's actual day:more enjoyable movement,a manageable break pattern,a consistent routine or support following an existing plan. Distinguish a request for a direct plan from a request for reflective coaching. Do not turn a simple scheduling question into a personality analysis or reopen an already chosen goal without reason.

Explore practical context:work/care duties,access,energy,environment,transport,enjoyment and competing priorities. A missed routine can reflect a structural barrier rather than low motivation. Let the person define success and acceptable effort. Avoid body judgment,streak shame or pressure to share sensitive details.

## Design a small experiment

Choose one feasible action with a concrete cue,location,duration or completion condition. Include an easier alternative for constrained days and a fallback when the normal cue fails. A small walking/wheeling break,comfortable household movement or an enjoyable activity can be more usable than an elaborate program the person cannot access.

State what will be tried,why it fits and when the person wants to review it. Keep the plan compatible with reported health restrictions and the shared coaching boundary. Do not prescribe a population activity target as an immediate requirement for a previously inactive person. Progress can mean repeating a manageable action,not increasing every variable.

## Review without blame

Compare intended action with the person's actual report. Ask what helped or obstructed it,and separate feasibility,enjoyment and outcome. A skipped activity does not require punishment or catch-up exercise. Adjust cue,environment,action size or timing based on the observed barrier rather than assuming a mindset defect.

Use a minimal chosen measure,such as completed opportunities or perceived ease,with clear dates and missing entries. A self-report is valid context but not objective proof of health change. Avoid false precision about habit formation time or a guaranteed number of days to automaticity.

## Deliver and respect ownership

Provide the chosen experiment,fallback and review question in a usable format. Offer reminders only when requested and supported by the host;do not create monitoring or contact a sponsor automatically. The person can pause,change direction or choose no action. Persistent distress,clinical issues or safeguarding concerns belong to appropriate support,not a habit-optimization loop.
