# General fitness planning

## Establish the training context

Identify the person's primary goal,current routine,experience,available days/session length,equipment and preferred activities. Use relevant recent activity and current readiness without requiring wearable data. Check reported symptoms and restrictions under the shared boundary before suggesting intensity. Do not infer cardiovascular fitness or injury status from age,appearance or an app score.

Choose a realistic starting plan that fits the actual week. Include purposeful aerobic activity,strength work and comfortable movement as relevant to the goal,with recovery and enjoyable alternatives. Population guidelines can frame a long-term direction;they do not establish an appropriate first week for everyone.

## Specify usable sessions

For each session state purpose,activity,approximate duration,effort cue,rest/recovery and an easier option. Include preparation appropriate to the chosen movement and avoid turning a short session into an impossible timetable. Count transitions and rest when estimating total duration. If exact loads or paces are unknown,use a conservative self-adjusted effort cue rather than inventing personalized numbers.

Explain how the person can gauge effort,for example conversational ease for routine aerobic work,while acknowledging individual variation. More demanding training requires suitable experience and context. Do not require maximal testing,high-intensity intervals or training to failure as a default for general fitness.

## Adapt from actual response

Review completed activity,perceived effort,recovery,enjoyment and any symptoms. Choose whether to repeat,reduce or gradually increase a relevant variable. Avoid a universal ten-percent rule or mandatory weekly improvement. A device trend can inform a question but cannot diagnose overtraining,illness or readiness for vigorous exercise.

If a session is missed,adjust the remaining week without stacking hard sessions to compensate. Persistent fatigue or symptoms require reassessment and potentially qualified advice. Keep food/body-composition discussion within general nonrestrictive education;specialized nutrition or medical treatment is outside this workflow.

## Deliver and evaluate

Provide a weekly overview and sufficient session detail to act,with assumptions,substitutions and review point. Use the person's preferred units and distinguish estimates from measured outcomes. A plan written,a wearable upload and a session completed are different states. No workout is actually performed or medically cleared by preparing this artifact.
