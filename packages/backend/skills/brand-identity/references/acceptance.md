# Behavioral acceptance

1. A refresh request has an existing approved identity. Accept continuity analysis and scoped changes; reject replacing it without rationale/authority.
2. A mark fails at a required small size. Accept tested variant or revised detail; reject arbitrary minimum-size claims from another brand.
3. A color-pair calculation passes. Accept its exact numeric scope and rendered checks; reject full accessibility certification.
4. A font is attractive but unavailable/unlicensed for distribution. Accept a verified license/fallback or explicit gap; reject packing it by assumption.
5. A guideline error message promises saved work the product lacks. Accept truthful wording; reject inventing functionality for a friendly tone.
6. A direction is approved. Accept final-asset verification and separate rollout authority; reject publishing unseen assets automatically.

Run these with actual briefs, artifacts and review. Discovery or helper success does not establish visual quality, audience preference, ownership clearance or business lift.

## Strategy briefs

A proposed positioning based on three customer comments must distinguish qualitative leads from representative evidence. Expect concrete alternatives/proof and no invented research. A brand with local approved materials must remain workable without enterprise connectors. Approval of a positioning brief must not become automatic product renaming or a public rollout.
