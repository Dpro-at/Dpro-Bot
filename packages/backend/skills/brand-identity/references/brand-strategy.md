# Brand strategy and positioning

## Evidence and decision

Identify the correct brand,actual business objective,offer,audience,category,markets,decision owner and existing commitments. Inspect approved strategy,voice,customer evidence,product facts and current applications before proposing a change. Document source/version and conflicting materials. One platform or local folder can contain sufficient evidence;absence of enterprise connectors does not block useful work.

Separate observed customer needs,internal belief,research hypothesis and approved positioning. A few comments are not a representative market study. Public comparisons must respect the brand's actual restrictions,including any ban on competitor naming. Research or asset access does not authorize scraping private communities,interviews or purchases.

## Strategic choices

Define the audience/job or problem,category frame,value promise,reason to believe and meaningful differentiation with proof. Include deliberate exclusions and tradeoffs:who the proposition is for,what it prioritizes and which unsupported claims it avoids. Adjectives like innovative or premium do not replace a concrete offer/choice.

Compare plausible positions against actual needs,product capability,commercial fit,evidence,continuity and delivery capacity. Do not infer market superiority or trademark clearance from originality. Keep audience segments from collapsing into invented stereotypes. Where portfolio/naming is in scope map product/masterbrand relationships and customer comprehension;do not rename live products implicitly.

## Messaging and validation

Translate the chosen proposal into a message hierarchy,proof points,tone rules,terminology and representative channel/application examples. A voice profile is one input,not the entire strategy. Show how each message traces to a factual promise and evidence. Separate testable hypotheses from approved claims.

Prepare an appropriately bounded validation plan:document review,actual customer comprehension,concept comparison or relevant channel experiment. Do not invent interview findings or declare audience lift from a designed concept. Paid testing,participant contact and public rollout require actual authorization.

## Handoff

Deliver the recommended position,alternatives/tradeoffs,source evidence,approved/proposed state,message examples and exact remaining decision. Reuse identity/application resources for visual/verbal execution. Keep private customer research and project-specific assets outside the general package.
