# Discovery and design direction

## Understand the existing identity

Inspect approved assets and representative touchpoints. Record which facts are established, which choices are current policy and which ideas were only proposed. A previous concept in a folder does not automatically represent approval. Identify continuity requirements before refreshing an identity that already has recognition.

Describe the audience and actual situation the brand serves. Use available evidence and label uncertainty; do not invent interviews, research results or market preference. Translate the offer into supported positioning rather than claiming universal leadership or fabricated differentiation.

## From strategy to criteria

Convert values into observable design/voice behavior. A useful criterion could require readable technical information, restrained use of the accent color, a recognizable silhouette at a target icon size or a particular level of verbal directness. Avoid choosing a popular archetype and treating it as proof of audience fit.

For a direction comparison, show how different concepts perform on the same required applications. State the concrete tradeoff: continuity versus change, expressive detail versus small-size clarity, type character versus platform availability. Do not assign invented numerical audience scores.

## Reference use

Study references for principles, structure and application problems. Do not copy distinctive marks, proprietary assets or another brand's claims. Follow the intended brand's public-comparison and naming restrictions. Where ownership or legal clearance matters, identify the required authoritative review rather than claiming a design is cleared because it looks original.

## Decision record

Capture the exact version reviewed, decision owner, approved direction, constraints and unresolved choices. Keep a rejected direction labeled as rejected. A stakeholder's approval of an idea does not approve unseen final exports or a public rollout. Preserve the decision so later work does not repeatedly reopen settled choices.
