# Process analysis and usable procedures

## Current state

Define the outcome,customer,trigger,start/end boundaries,inputs,outputs,participants,systems and policy constraints. Read existing procedures and actual work records;distinguish documented intention from observed practice. Map normal paths,decision conditions,handoffs,queues,exceptions,rework and stop/escalation behavior. Do not assume a process is broken because a document is missing.

Record where evidence comes from:walkthrough,self-report,transaction sample or observed execution. Identify missing variants and actual scope. A diagram can be correct for one team/site while incomplete elsewhere. Avoid inventing staff roles or mandatory approval steps unrelated to the actual process.

## Analyze performance

Define throughput,cycle time,working time,wait time,backlog,error/rework and completion under a common period/population. Use appropriate distributions and denominators;averages can hide long-tail waits. Identify bottleneck hypotheses and evidence that would distinguish demand,capacity,dependency,quality or policy causes. A slow step is not automatically the system constraint.

Compare options against the actual outcome,cost,control/access requirements,capacity and failure modes. Removing a control may reduce recorded cycle time while increasing risk or downstream work. Automating a poor step does not prove the process improved. Keep current-state evidence,target proposal and owner-approved change separate.

## SOP and pilot

Write each step with trigger,role,input,action,decision/output,evidence and failure route. Use actual tool/source locations and named role ownership where supported. Include handoff acceptance,exceptions and recovery,not just the happy path. Reuse the existing document home and version/review conventions.

Propose a bounded pilot with a baseline,success/stop criteria,participants/authority and comparable observation window. Do not contact staff,change systems,assign work or publish a new mandatory procedure implicitly. A walkthrough can validate readability and missing branches;actual adoption and outcome improvement require operating evidence.

## Deliverable

Provide the useful current-state map,measured gaps versus hypotheses,proposed future state,complete procedure and owned closure checks. Report what was observed/tested,scope/sample limits and decisions still required. A new SOP or filled RACI is not proof of execution,accountability acceptance or causal performance gain.
