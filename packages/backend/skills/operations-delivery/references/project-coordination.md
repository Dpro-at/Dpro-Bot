# Project coordination and status

## Plan from actual commitments

Identify project/outcome,scope,owner,stakeholders,baseline milestones,acceptance criteria and actual tracker. Separate deliverables,tasks,decisions,risks,issues and dependencies. Reuse stable IDs and update the existing source rather than creating a duplicate plan. A task title or percent complete does not define acceptance.

For each item record owner,dependency,status,evidence,needed-by date,estimate versus commitment and next action. Check resource/calendar/timezone assumptions proportionately. Do not assign real people or move deadlines without actual authority. Unknown duration or ownership stays explicit;do not manufacture a precise finish date from incomplete inputs.

## Track and reconcile

Inspect actual work/acceptance evidence and recent changes. Distinguish started,implementation complete,review pending,accepted,released and blocked as the project uses them. Ticket closure is not necessarily delivered value. Resolve duplicate or contradictory tracker/doc/chat updates through current source and owner evidence rather than silently overwriting.

Analyze dependency effects and critical sequencing when data supports it. Explain slippage against the actual baseline,forecast assumptions and available options. A risk is uncertain future impact;an issue is occurring now. Record mitigation,decision authority and trigger rather than turning every uncertainty into a red status.

## Status and decisions

Lead with outcome trajectory,material changes,actual accomplishments and what needs a decision. Ground health labels in agreed criteria;do not infer green from a lack of updates. A concise status should separate facts,estimates and unsupported assumptions,with exact source/as-of and remaining evidence.

For decisions provide context,options,tradeoff,recommendation,owner and time sensitivity. For follow-up identify the smallest concrete next action and needed input. Meeting scheduling,messages,task creation,assignment and external reports require actual authority;draft the useful agenda/report first where appropriate.

## Closure

Verify deliverable acceptance against the agreed scope and record unresolved defects,operational handoff and ownership. Do not call a project done solely from all tasks closed. Preserve baseline/change history and actual decision receipts. Report remaining acceptance,resource constraints and next owner actions without pretending that a coordinated plan was executed.
