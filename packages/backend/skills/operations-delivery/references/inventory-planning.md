# Inventory planning and reconciliation

## State and demand

Define SKU/variant,location,unit of measure,as-of time and inventory status. Distinguish physical on-hand,allocated/reserved,quarantined,damaged,available,in transit and confirmed inbound quantities. State how backorders,returns,transfers and cancellations affect the decision. Do not subtract the same allocation twice or treat every inbound order as usable on the needed date.

Reconcile opening stock plus relevant receipts/returns/adjustments minus shipments/consumption/transfers to closing stock under the declared location/unit boundary. A sales export is not automatically a stock movement ledger. Record unexplained variance and last physical count;do not silently post an adjustment to make totals agree.

Estimate demand using relevant actual history,availability,promotions,seasonality,large orders and lead-time variability. A stockout interval censors observed sales and is not proof of zero demand. New or intermittent items can need judgment/scenario bounds rather than a precise daily rate. Zero recorded sales does not universally mean never replenish:critical spare parts,contract commitments or a documented seasonal requirement may differ.

## Replenishment decision

Use the actual service/cash/storage objective,review cycle,lead time,safety-stock policy,usable stock and reliable inbound. A useful declared inventory position can be usable on-hand plus eligible inbound minus commitments/backorders,provided definitions avoid double counting. Reorder points or target-stock quantities depend on policy and uncertainty;do not copy fixed day windows or cover targets as universal defaults.

For a declared target policy calculate the net requirement explicitly,apply order/pack/minimum constraints and show the rounding consequence. Compare arrival dates against need dates and quantify cost,currency and cash exposure. Do not invent lead times,supplier capacity or exact stockout dates. Present scenarios when the demand basis or arrival is unreliable.

## Exceptions and action

Separate excess/obsolete/damaged stock from normal reorder proposals with policy-based rationale. Inventory carrying value,expected sale value and purchase cost are different measures. A recommendation to discount,dispose or write down inventory is not authority to do so. Validate regulated/perishable/lot/expiry requirements with actual domain policy when relevant.

Deliver a prioritized buy/hold/investigate list with inputs,formula,confidence limits,owner and next observation. Drafting a PO can be useful,but creating/issuing it,sending vendor email,changing stock or booking spend needs applicable authority. Recheck current item/location/order state and reconcile unknown results before retry.
