# Editorial handoff

## Deliverable
Channel, audience, draft version and file location.

## Review status
What was checked, what was not checked and any blocking claim or asset issue.

## Evidence and assumptions
Direct references for consequential claims; explain any inference or fictional example.

## Presentation needs
Required visual assets, alt-text suggestions, link destinations and outstanding permissions.

## Publication state
Draft, ready for review or explicitly approved for the requested action. Do not infer approval from completion of the draft.
