# Email marketing and lifecycle sequences

## Audience and entry contract

Identify brand,sender/account,objective,audience/lifecycle stage,recipient eligibility,source of permission,platform and desired deliverable. Distinguish transactional communication from promotional content under the actual policy/jurisdiction. Do not infer permission from possession of addresses,a CRM export or prior purchase alone. Use approved suppression and preference rules and current applicable requirements;do not embed one universal legal deadline.

Inspect current campaigns,sequence IDs,templates and recipient definitions before creating another. Avoid exporting recipient-level data unnecessarily. Define entry trigger,event/attribute source,eligibility,exclusions,frequency/quiet hours,timezone,re-entry and completion. Keep missing audience or suppression evidence explicit rather than treating unknown as eligible.

## Sequence and copy

Choose the number and spacing of messages from the actual objective,value progression,recipient context and capacity. Do not adopt universal day counts or benchmark ranges. Every step needs purpose,subject/preheader,full usable body,primary action/link,personalization fallback,delay basis and skip/exit condition. A subject must not fake a reply,deadline or personal relationship.

Use supported claims,prices,offer conditions and real proof. Personalization should remain meaningful when a field is absent;do not expose raw tokens or sensitive attributes. Validate destination links,tracking intent,accessibility,plain-text usefulness and the actual mobile/template rendering when available. A chat draft is not an inspected rendered email.

## Logic and suppression

Define precedence when events arrive late or concurrently:conversion,unsubscribe,bounce/complaint,eligibility change and another active sequence can affect later sends. An open event can be noisy or privacy-mediated;do not equate it with human intent or use it as unquestioned proof for a branch. Record which event actually signals the desired outcome.

Apply current suppression and entry state again at the platform's supported decision boundary before an authorized send. A saved audience snapshot can become stale. Use stable recipient/sequence-step identity and the platform's actual idempotency/reconciliation mechanisms. A timeout after submission is an unknown result,not permission to resend blindly.

## Delivery and learning

Review current official sender/platform guidance for authentication,alignment,unsubscribe and reputation requirements in the actual scope. Do not change DNS,sender identity,lists or automation without applicable authority. A configured domain or accepted message does not prove inbox placement,human reading or conversion.

Drafting can finish with complete copy,logic and setup/validation notes even without a connector. Creating a platform draft,enrolling recipients,activating a flow,scheduling and sending are distinct external actions. Respect actual session authority and do not add unnecessary reapproval for already authorized work.

Measure sent,accepted,delivered,bounced,complained,unsubscribed,clicked and converted under explicit definitions/windows. Separate platform events from people and attributed outcomes from causal lift. Use data-analysis for reconciled campaign measurement. Report actual tests,remaining rendering/platform/suppression/delivery acceptance and next owner action.
