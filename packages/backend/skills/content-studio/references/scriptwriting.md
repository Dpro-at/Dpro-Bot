# Scriptwriting for performance

## Define the medium and through-line

Identify audience,format,objective,voice,fiction/nonfiction status,length and production constraints. Narration,interview guide,documentary edit script,dramatic dialogue,presentation and product demo need different structures. Read approved facts,source excerpts,brand and existing draft before choosing beats;do not impose one promotional formula on every genre.

Create a coherent progression with meaningful change,explanation or tension appropriate to the purpose. Link nonfiction claims,quotes and examples to actual sources. An interview question is not a guest answer;a proposed reconstruction is not documentary evidence. Label fictional/composite material and do not invent testimonials,personal experience,statistics or product capability for drama.

## Write usable performance text

Use clear referents,pronounceable terms,natural rhythm and distinct character/host voices for first-hearing comprehension. Sentence length,pauses,repetition and transitions are choices,not universal word caps or mandatory filler. Match the actual requested language/culture;maintained workflow resources remain English.

Separate speech from visuals,actions,sound and production notes. Use stable scene/line IDs and cue references. Include concrete direction needed for production without overprescribing every camera move. Choose a suitable format:two-column AV may serve an explainer,while dramatic/audio work may need other conventions.

## Timing and revisions

Estimate speech pace plus pauses,demonstrations,music and visual reading time under declared assumptions. Word count cannot establish actual runtime,emotion or comprehension. Read aloud or use an authorized scratch performance when available,and record measured sections. Never claim that test ran from a text-only review.

Revise purpose,clarity,voice,beat continuity,source accuracy and feasibility together. Check names,numbers,pronunciations,quotes and cue consistency. Let visuals carry appropriate information rather than overloading dialogue. Preserve exact approved versions;changed factual claims or assets can invalidate related approval.

## Deliver

Provide full requested script,beat/cue structure,source/rights notes,timing basis and actual review results. A script is not a recorded interview,rendered clip or produced episode. Use visual/audio workflows for execution. Paid speech,voice cloning,participant contact and publication require actual authority and rights.
