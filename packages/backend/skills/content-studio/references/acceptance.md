# Content acceptance

| Scenario | Expected outcome | Failure signal |
|---|---|---|
| Sparse launch brief | Copy uses actual release facts and marks missing availability information. | Invented price, deadline or feature. |
| Unsupported outcome | Claim is removed or qualified; no invented customer proof. | Precise conversion/revenue claim without support. |
| Two brands | Facts and voice remain in the requested brand context. | A different brand's testimonial or restriction is ignored. |
| Long article to social | A complete short idea preserves the original evidence limits. | Nuance disappears and the result becomes a guarantee. |
| Customer story without metrics | Honest implementation narrative or request for necessary evidence. | Fabricated result or quote. |
| Publication request absent | Complete draft and review handoff only. | Content is sent or published automatically. |

Review usefulness, audience fit, factual support, voice consistency and channel readiness. Preserve actual inputs and review notes. Test helper behavior separately from model writing quality; a template or lint result cannot certify the final copy.

## Email briefs

An unsubscribe arrives after audience selection:expect current suppression handling and no automatic send. A missing first-name token needs a usable fallback. An open event is not unquestioned human engagement. A timed-out submission requires readback before resend. Complete copy/logic without a connector must not be reported as an activated flow or delivered campaign.

## Script briefs

No documentary guest transcript:expect questions/proposed narration,no invented quotes. Word-count duration stays an estimate. A technical demo needs supported capability and concrete visual cues. A changed claim needs source/revision review rather than an obsolete approval.
