# Voice performance and take direction

## Performance contract

Identify script/version,character or narrator,language/dialect,audience,medium,purpose,duration constraints and actual delivery. Read the supported facts and pronunciation requirements. Separate a real person's recorded speech,fictional role and synthetic voice;do not impersonate endorsements or fabricate testimony. Voice likeness/cloning and use rights follow actual consent and host/provider rules.

Describe playable direction:who is addressed,what the speaker wants,what changes through the passage,emphasis,pace,pauses and emotional progression. Avoid stereotyped assumptions from gender,nationality or accent. A generic adjective such as warm is not a complete direction;include a relevant line-level intention or contrast when useful.

## Prepare and audition

Mark names,numbers,acronyms,technical terms and pronunciation choices. Preserve meaning when expanding symbols for speech;keep the approved readable script and any performance text aligned. Use stable scene/line/take IDs and note intentional overlap or breaths. Estimated word rate is planning,not measured duration or performance quality.

For actual recording use an authorized short audition and suitable capture procedure. For requested synthetic speech check current supported model/voice/language/style/input limits and cost scope. Do not copy a stale voice catalog or assume one API is best/cheapest for every task. Provider-neutral instructions do not imply every provider exposes identical controls.

Listen to the actual audition for pronunciation,meaning,emotion,consistency,intelligibility and fit with the visual/audio context. A successful job or waveform is not a performance review. If playback is unavailable,report it and deliver useful direction without claiming approval. Paid generation or repeated variants need the actual authorization and bounded retry/cost handling.

## Produce consistent takes

Track script revision,voice identity,tool/model/settings and accepted reference take. Keep continuity across chunks and scenes while allowing intentional performance variation. Prompt/seed settings do not guarantee identical voice or prosody. Check chunk joins,missing/repeated words,speaker swaps and timing drift before selecting a take.

Do not rewrite approved factual claims to make synthesis easier without review. If exact duration is required,adjust the script or performance under the actual authority;speed manipulation can affect intelligibility/character and is not equivalent to a new performance. Preserve original takes and actual selection rationale.

## Handoff

Deliver accepted takes or a complete direction/audition packet with script IDs,pronunciation notes,estimated versus measured timing,rights and actual listening scope. Use sound-post-production for technical repair/mix. A rendered take,approved performance,final master and public release remain distinct. No model download,voice clone,paid API or publication follows from loading the skill.
