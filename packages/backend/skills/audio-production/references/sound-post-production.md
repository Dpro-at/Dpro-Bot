# Sound editing and post-production

## Diagnose the material

Identify project/source versions,tracks,recording context,content purpose,destination and actual edit authority. Preserve originals and inspect metadata plus representative listening where available. Separate noise,hum,plosives,clicks,reverb,clipping,phase/sync,level inconsistency and intentional ambience. A microphone label or speaker's gender is not enough to choose EQ or dynamics parameters.

Locate problems by track/source time and distinguish constant from intermittent conditions. Compare source and processed audio at suitably matched listening levels so a louder candidate is not mistaken for a better one. Record unheard sections and unavailable tooling;do not infer a clean recording from a waveform or file probe alone.

## Edit and repair with a reason

Choose the smallest process that addresses the actual problem:manual clip repair,level automation,appropriate filtering,de-essing,noise treatment,dynamics or a new take. Processing order depends on signal and purpose;there is no universally mandatory chain. Avoid fixed thresholds,frequency bands or silence lengths copied from another voice/room. A noise profile must represent the actual noise,not speech that the algorithm will remove.

Check each intervention for intelligibility,artifacts,breath/turn continuity and preserved meaning. Noise reduction or source separation can fail;do not promise restoration of information absent from a clipped/mixed recording. A gate can suppress gaps but cannot generally remove reverberation under speech. When original stems or a re-recording would materially improve the result,explain that option and current limits.

For multitrack work preserve timing and intended channel relationships. Dialogue,ambience,foley,effects and music have different roles;do not normalize intentional quiet atmosphere to a speech target. Edit choices must preserve real speaker meaning and consent/rights. Keep a source/timeline/processing log so changes can be reviewed or reversed.

## Measurement and perceptual checks

Define actual delivery criteria and units before adjusting levels. Integrated/short-term loudness,LRA,RMS,sample peak,true peak and noise-floor measurements are not interchangeable. Select a supported meter/method and relevant measurement scope. A short sample can diagnose a defect but does not establish whole-program integrated results. A metadata tag or successful filter run is not an independent output measurement.

RMS-based audiobook requirements must not be relabeled as LUFS. Verify the actual current destination specification,channel/codec constraints and intended listening use. Do not present one set of podcast/video/mastering numbers as universal standards or claim every short program measurement is invalid below an arbitrary duration.

After export inspect technical output and actual playback of joins,quiet/loud sections,beginning/end and the requested scope. Lossy encoding can change peak behavior,so check the delivered version where required. Record measurements separately from subjective listening observations. Passing a meter does not prove freedom from overprocessing or recipient approval.

## Deliver

Provide actual edited audio/session/stems and a scoped receipt when authorized and available;otherwise deliver a concrete repair/mix plan with target timecodes,decision rationale and missing checks. Installing tools,paid denoising/synthesis,external uploads and publication need actual authority. Original private recordings and licenses remain outside portable packages.
