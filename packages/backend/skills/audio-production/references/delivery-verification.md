# Delivery,verification and release

Define destination and actual outputs:session/stems,master,distribution file,transcript,chapters,show notes,credits/artwork and release metadata as requested. Platform requirements can differ for RSS versus subscriber uploads and format/channel configurations. Check official current guidance rather than applying universal codec/loudness defaults.

Verify actual file presence,format,channels,sample rate,duration,decode/playback and relevant measured loudness/true peak. Inspect beginning,end,joins and representative quiet/loud speech,plus full listening when practical. Decode success detects some technical failures but cannot prove editorial quality,pronunciation or rights. Record exactly what was heard and unobserved sections.

Check final transcript alignment,names,links,claims,credits and chapter positions after editing. Show notes must not invent quotes,resources or sponsorship. Estimated duration,rendered duration and published-player behavior are distinct.

Deliver the inspected audio or full production packet with source/version,review findings and limits. Upload,hosting/RSS changes,notifications and publication require applicable authority. Upload acceptance is not necessarily a published accessible episode. Reconcile uncertain submissions before retry using stable episode/version identity.

Keep private/raw recordings and credentials outside portable settings. Real guest/editor/listener/platform and model-task acceptance remain separate from native package parsing/discovery and library trials.
