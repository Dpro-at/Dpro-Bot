# People operations

Deliver a useful people-process work product grounded in the actual organization,policy and authorized records. Distinguish a prepared plan,a verified record,a proposed recommendation and an executed employment action. A connector or generated checklist does not grant access,approve a benefit or decide who is hired.

Read [Runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md) in a new host. Maintained source and templates remain English. Inspect supplied policies,role requirements,existing workflows and permitted source records. Use the task's organization,location,effective date and employment context;do not import another jurisdiction's rule or a generic handbook default.

## Select the process

- For a new team member,read [Employee onboarding](references/onboarding.md).
- For policy explanation,record correction or an operational HR case,read [People process and policy](references/process-policy.md).
- For sourcing/interview/pipeline support,read [Recruitment evidence](references/recruitment.md).
- For workforce metrics or reporting,read [People reporting](references/people-reporting.md).

Read [Records and authority](references/records-authority.md) for any private employee/candidate data or external action. Use [Process brief](assets/process-brief.md) to establish the actual task and data boundary. The package is self-contained;it does not require a particular HR platform or another skill.

## Ground the work

Identify the person/object by an authorized stable reference where needed,without exposing unnecessary personal data. Confirm source revision,policy scope and effective period. Keep observed facts,employee/candidate statements,reviewer interpretation and unresolved questions distinct.

Use existing role/process templates before creating a new one. Make owners,dependencies,expected evidence and status explicit. Do not mark access granted,training completed,an offer accepted or a policy exception approved merely because it appears in a plan.

For recruitment,summarize relevant qualifications against the role's actual criteria. Keep unknown or unassessed evidence visible. Do not infer protected traits,health,personality or trustworthiness from names,photos,accents or unrelated personal information. Do not automatically reject,hire,promote or otherwise make a consequential employment decision from a generated score.

## Review and execute only within scope

Use [Onboarding task map](assets/onboarding-map.md),[Policy case](assets/policy-case.md) or [Interview and pipeline record](assets/interview-pipeline.md). Preserve human decision ownership and the existing service's permission/version controls. Calendar invitations,messages,HRIS changes,access grants and ATS stage changes retain their applicable authorization.

Check meaningful completion evidence rather than a filled checkbox. Keep private notes/reports limited to the intended audience. Handle conflicting/missing policy through a named owner or unresolved item,not an invented entitlement or legal conclusion.

## Deliver

Return the requested plan,case summary,interview kit or report with sources,status and exact remaining action. Do not activate integrations,send messages or create accounts merely to make the work look complete. [Acceptance briefs](references/acceptance.md) define future trials;[Design notes](references/design-notes.md) record upstream coverage. Actual HR-system/participant acceptance and model-task quality are separate from package discovery.
