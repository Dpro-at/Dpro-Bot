# Recruitment evidence and workflow

Start from an authorized requisition and current job description. Identify essential tasks,required qualifications,location/work constraints,decision owner and existing evaluation process. Do not infer hiring criteria from the demographics of current staff or a vague cultural-fit preference.

## Structured evidence

Define relevant competencies and observable evidence before comparing candidates. Use job-related work samples,experience and supplied responses. An absent record is unknown,not a proven lack of ability. Distinguish a candidate's statement,verified evidence and interviewer interpretation. A numerical score should have an explained rubric and must not hide uncertainty.

Prepare questions and follow-ups that elicit the intended evidence consistently,with appropriate access/accommodation under the actual process. Do not use names,photos,accents,health information or unrelated personal details to infer capability,personality or trustworthiness. Do not rank or exclude candidates on protected traits. Keep demographic monitoring separate from individual selection support and within authorized policy.

## Interview kit

Use the interview/pipeline template for role criteria,question purpose,evidence anchors and unassessed areas. Avoid a fixed question count or rating scale when the existing process specifies another. A panel plan names proposed responsibilities;it does not assign real people or send invitations automatically.

Review whether each criterion measures an actual job need and whether the evidence supports the conclusion. A polished resume or fluent response is not proof of every required skill. Do not use an AI-generated score as an automatic rejection,offer or employment decision. Keep a responsible human owner for consequential decisions.

## Pipeline

Use the organization's actual stage definitions and authoritative candidate/requisition IDs. Record entry/exit meaning,dates,source version and withdrawn/closed states. A pending interview is not a completed evaluation. Candidate communication,ATS mutations and calendar changes require their applicable authorization;connector availability is not consent.

Metrics need a defined cohort,period,denominator and status handling. Stage conversion,time-to-fill and offer acceptance can differ under different definitions. Keep unknown dates and duplicate candidates explicit. Avoid presenting an aggregate correlation as the reason an individual should be selected.

## Domain boundary

The [EEOC selection-procedure guidance](https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/employment-tests-and-selection-procedures) provides a US example of job-relatedness and discrimination concerns. It is not a universal jurisdictional checklist. Confirm the actual applicable policy/law with authoritative current sources when needed. This package does not validate a test,certify fairness or make final employment decisions.
