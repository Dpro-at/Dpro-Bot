The upstream supplies compact onboarding,policy,reporting,pipeline and interview workflows. This package adds self-contained source/effective-scope,record/authority,job-related evidence,unknown-status and actual-execution boundaries. It does not reproduce default timetables,metric catalogs or connector automation. It avoids automatic invitations/stage updates,unsupported individual risk labels and treating a generated checklist as verified completion. It does not validate a selection instrument,certify compliance or make a final employment decision. The upstream remains broader in its full HR platform beyond these selected entrypoints.

No matched model-task superiority or employee-outcome claim. Native loading and isolated library checks are preliminary evidence;actual policy/domain/system/participant acceptance remains separate. Behavioral briefs are future inputs,not executed results.
