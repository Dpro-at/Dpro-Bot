# People reporting

Begin with the decision/question and permitted audience. Define organization/entity,as-of date or observation window,worker population,status categories and source version. Do not collect every available personal field merely because it might be interesting later.

## Population and measures

Choose a stable person/assignment reference and resolve duplicates,transfers,rehire and multiple-role cases according to the actual report. Distinguish headcount,FTE,open requisitions and assignments. Specify how starters,leavers,contractors and leave are treated. An unexplained count can be numerically correct while answering the wrong question.

For attrition or conversion,define numerator,denominator,time window and cohort. Keep missing or partial history visible. Explain when small populations or changing definitions make a trend unstable. Compare like periods and scope rather than inferring a change from different extracts.

## Privacy and inference

Use only authorized fields and aggregation appropriate to the audience. Small groups,free-text comments and combinations of attributes can reveal individuals even when names are omitted. Apply the organization's actual disclosure controls rather than inventing a universal group-size threshold.

Keep observation and interpretation separate. A survey score,work-system log or tenure metric cannot by itself establish an individual's productivity,motivation,health or likelihood of leaving. Do not generate unsupported personal flight-risk labels or employment recommendations from unrelated behavior. Aggregate analysis still needs a legitimate purpose and careful source interpretation.

If demographic analysis is explicitly authorized for a valid monitoring purpose,retain correct definitions and restricted access. Do not turn those attributes into individual hiring/promotion criteria. Avoid broad collection or reuse under a new purpose without the actual policy/authority review.

## Evidence and delivery

Tie reported totals to the intended source population and document exclusions. Review labels,units,dates,formulae and uncertainty. Use synthetic examples for templates;do not fabricate employee records or survey responses to make a report appear complete.

Deliver a concise answer,method,source scope and remaining gaps. Sharing a report externally or in a team channel is a separate action requiring authorization. A generated report is not automatically approved for all managers or a basis for consequential employee action.
