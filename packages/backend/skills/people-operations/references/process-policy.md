# People process and policy

Identify the actual question,affected population,organization,location,effective date and source policy. Use current supplied/authorized documents and the correct version. A policy for one subsidiary,worker category or country may not apply to another.

## Explain from evidence

Find the relevant section and distinguish the policy's wording from a plain-language explanation. Preserve conditions,exceptions,approval requirements and effective dates. Quote only what is needed and cite a usable document/section reference. Do not fabricate a policy,contact name,balance,benefit election or legal entitlement.

If documents conflict,record the conflicting versions and the decision owner. If the record is missing,explain the precise gap and finish independent parts. A generic internet source can inform a legal/policy question but cannot establish the organization's internal rule. Current legal interpretation requires the relevant jurisdiction and authoritative sources;the package does not supply universal employment-law answers.

## Operational cases

For a record correction or process request,name the current state,requested change,authority owner,required evidence and affected downstream systems. Preserve original provenance and version history where supported. A data-entry correction is different from changing the underlying approved decision.

Separate a proposal from an authorized HRIS mutation. Verify the current employee/object and permission rather than trusting a display name or stale exported row. Keep unrelated fields unchanged. After an actual permitted write,read back the relevant state and retain the appropriate audit reference without exposing secrets.

## Sensitive or consequential issues

Use the organization's actual route for grievances,accommodations,discipline,pay changes or other sensitive cases. Limit access to those who need the information for the task. Do not infer health,protected characteristics or intent from sparse records. A summary may identify missing evidence or next procedural steps without making a final employment or legal judgment.

Do not automatically send a case to a third party or publish an employee-specific answer. A request to explain policy does not authorize sharing the underlying private file. Deliver the concise source-grounded answer,exception/status and exact owner action needed.
