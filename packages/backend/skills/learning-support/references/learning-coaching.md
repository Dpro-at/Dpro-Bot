# Learning coaching

Turn a learner's goal into a feasible next sequence of work. Start from what they need to do,their actual availability,the material they have and evidence of their current performance. A plan should fit the learner's constraints rather than advertise a guaranteed transformation.

## Define the target

Make the goal observable:solve a named class of problems,explain a mechanism,write a small program or perform a workplace task. Separate knowledge/skill gaps from missing access,unclear expectations,unavailable tools or an overloaded schedule. Do not assume that every missed deadline means poor motivation or a medical/psychological condition.

Inspect existing syllabus,notes,assessment feedback and prior attempts where authorized. Identify a few meaningful dependencies. A prerequisite may need brief support in context rather than a whole course completed first. Avoid a rigid ladder that prevents any useful task until every lower-level fact is marked mastered.

## Build a usable plan

Choose a small coherent milestone and define what evidence will show it. Estimate effort for reading,attempts,feedback and revision,not only watching content. Leave room for actual availability and state uncertain estimates. If the goal/time combination is unrealistic,explain the scope tradeoff without inventing a precise universal hours-to-mastery number.

Include retrieval or application where it supports the objective,and revisit useful material in varied contexts. Review timing can adapt to availability and observed difficulty;do not claim a scheduling formula prevents forgetting. A proposed review date is different from an active reminder.

Use [Study plan](../assets/study-plan.md) for an authorized artifact. Keep links to relevant source material and existing work rather than copying private content into a global profile. Avoid planning an enormous curriculum before the learner has tested the first meaningful step.

## Adjust from evidence

After a milestone,compare intended and observed work. Distinguish independent success,success with a model,task ambiguity and an environmental blocker. Revise the smallest useful part of the plan. A missed attempt may justify a different explanation or task size,not an identity judgment about the learner.

Track progress in task terms rather than decorative mastery badges unsupported by evidence. If the learner asks for a dashboard,show what is observed,uncertain and not assessed. Persist only authorized minimal notes. Do not infer a cognitive diagnosis or make the coaching plan a substitute for professional support outside the task.
