# Progress and evidence

Track the relevant performance,not a flattering label. Use a minimal record of the goal,task,attempt,support and observed result under the host's data policy. Keep private source material,personal stories and unnecessary identifiers out of portable records.

## Evidence categories

- Independent performance:the learner completes the relevant task without the supplied answer or material hint under the stated conditions.
- Assisted performance:the learner succeeds after a hint,worked model,step selection or substantial correction. Record the help.
- Content delivered:an explanation,example or plan was provided,but no learner performance was observed.
- Not assessed:the skill or condition was not exercised.

These categories are local observations,not permanent identity judgments. A copied correction may be useful practice but should not be reported as independent transfer. One successful case does not guarantee long-term retention or performance in another domain.

## Feedback records

Preserve the learner's intended reasoning and identify the narrow issue. If multiple explanations fit the error,state the uncertainty or use a targeted follow-up. Do not infer intelligence,learning disability,motivation or a stable learning style from a small sample. Preferences can guide communication without becoming diagnostic facts.

When revisiting material,vary the meaningful context while keeping the target skill recognizable. Compare the support and conditions across attempts. Avoid a percentage or mastery badge that hides sparse evidence or different task difficulty. If a score is useful,define the criteria and what it does not measure.

## Persistence and scheduling

Use an existing authorized memory or session record when it adds value. Do not create global directories/profile files automatically or save every mistake indefinitely. Corrections should supersede stale notes with appropriate provenance. Honor the user's retention/export choices.

A proposed review plan is not an active reminder. Scheduling requires an available authorized capability;without it,deliver a usable plan and say what remains manual. No formula or notification cadence guarantees that a learner will never forget.

## Quality claims

Native skill parsing and discovery establish loading. A deterministic calculation or code example establishes only the executed case. Actual model-task teaching behavior and learner improvement require relevant observed trials. Compare a baseline and candidate on matched tasks if claiming a quality gain;do not substitute resource length or number of files for learning evidence.
