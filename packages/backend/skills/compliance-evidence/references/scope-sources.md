# Scope,applicability and source control

## Define the question

Identify the initiative or process,entity/business role,systems,data flows,locations,affected people,customer commitments and decision date. State the requested output:triage,requirement map,control evidence review,readiness gap register or response preparation. Keep exclusions and the reason visible. Do not expand a narrow feature review into an organization-wide audit without need and authority.

Clarify which requirements are law,regulatory guidance,contractual obligation,organization policy or voluntary framework. These have different applicability and decision owners. Geography or a keyword alone does not prove legal coverage. Record provisional relevance and the fact needed to resolve it;ask the actual owner/reviewer rather than making an unsupported determination.

## Source register

For each material criterion record issuing body,title,version/effective date,section/reference,official location,access date and relevant scope. Use current authoritative sources for legal/regulatory or time-sensitive requirements. Distinguish proposed guidance,future effective provisions and current obligations. If paid/licensed clause text is unavailable,request the permitted source or keep exact clause mapping unresolved;do not fabricate or redistribute protected standards.

Preserve the actual meaning,conditions and exceptions in a concise requirement statement. Avoid converting relative terms or calendar periods into invented fixed deadlines. A prior policy summary can guide discovery but is not necessarily the current controlling source. Track source conflict and responsible interpretation separately.

## Practical intake

Map data entering,stored,processed,shared,logged,backed up and deleted at a level proportionate to the question. Identify system/process owners and existing records. Use minimal necessary information and safe references rather than copying whole databases or contracts. A questionnaire answer is self-report until corroborated by suitable evidence.

Deliver the scoped question,source register,confirmed facts,assumptions and unresolved applicability decisions. A useful draft can be complete as preparation while formal applicability or approval remains open.
