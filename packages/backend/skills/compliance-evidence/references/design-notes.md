# Design notes and comparison

Complete selected entrypoint reviewed: [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/blob/33d3dea41a341b341d4f5da2e8801ae0c89be2b8/legal/skills/compliance-check/SKILL.md),commit `33d3dea41a341b341d4f5da2e8801ae0c89be2b8`. Pinned source and hashes are private. A search result's former compliance-audit mirror path returned404 at repository HEAD;the review switched to this verified primary upstream. The unavailable mirror was not treated as reviewed. Linked upstream connector resources were not exhaustively reviewed.

The source offers practical initiative triage,privacy/DPA/request-handling context and regulatory-monitoring ideas. Our independent package covers general scope/source control,criterion/evidence/period traceability,design-versus-operation,gap/remediation closure and records/action authority. It does not copy legal scope summaries,deadline tables,contract positions,control IDs or external-service recipes. Current official requirements and actual qualified decisions must be obtained for a substantive jurisdictional task.

The tradeoff is less prewritten jurisdiction-specific guidance and no connector automation. The package provides full usable evidence-preparation resources instead of generic green scores or a legal opinion. No matched model task,formal auditor acceptance or compliance superiority was measured. Original prompts,text,examples and templates are not redistributed.

[NIST's Cybersecurity Framework overview](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) was checked as an example of risk-management framework resources and mappings,not a universal law or certification determination. No framework clause text is bundled. A mapping or template cannot establish equivalent obligations or implemented operation.

No executable helper was needed;native source/record analysis and substantive reviewer evidence are more meaningful than counting files or searching keywords. English/link checks,native bundle parsing/discovery and isolated library-preservation trials are preparation evidence only. Actual legal/domain/model-task and live-system acceptance remain separate.
