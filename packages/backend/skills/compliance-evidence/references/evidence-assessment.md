# Requirement and evidence assessment

## Traceability

Use actual requirement/control identifiers from the selected source. Map each material requirement to responsible process/system,implementation claim,evidence reference,period/population and owner. Do not copy one reassuring policy paragraph across unrelated controls. Cross-framework mappings can suggest overlap but do not prove equivalent scope or satisfaction.

Distinguish design evidence from operation:a policy describes intended behavior;a configuration snapshot shows a point in time;a log or record may show specific execution;an independent report has its own scope and limitations. Record source identity,version,time,collection method and authorized access. Hashes establish byte identity to the captured object,not truth,completeness or effective operation.

## Assessment method

State the criterion and observation needed before inspecting. Choose a proportionate procedure:document inspection,configuration review,walkthrough,sample of operating records or authorized deterministic check. Define the relevant period,population,sampling rationale and exclusions. If the population is incomplete,say so;absence in a sample is not proof of absence everywhere.

Classify evidence as observed,supplied assertion,missing,stale,conflicting or not assessed. Classify a requirement only as far as the evidence supports:design appears present,scoped operation observed,gap supported or unresolved. Avoid a blanket green percentage that treats missing evidence as success or assumes every control is equally important.

## Findings

A finding needs the actual criterion,source evidence,comparison,consequence and uncertainty. Distinguish an evidenced implementation gap from a missing-evidence gap. If a control may exist in a shared platform,record where you looked and the specific ownership/reference needed. Do not manufacture deficiencies from a source-only omission.

Keep cause hypotheses separate from established facts. A passing technical scan or validator is scoped evidence;it does not establish legal compliance,complete risk management or a formal audit opinion. Do not invent statistical confidence or certification readiness from document counts.
