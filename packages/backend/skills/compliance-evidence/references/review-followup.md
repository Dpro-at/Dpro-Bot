# Review,remediation and closure

## Make the next decision concrete

Summarize the scoped question,evidence reviewed,material supported gaps,unknowns and responsible decisions. Prioritize using actual consequence,likelihood/exposure,time sensitivity,dependencies and the owner's risk model. Do not import universal severity labels or a mandatory release block from another organization.

For each action define a concrete outcome,owner,dependency,target context,authority and closure evidence. Separate preparing a policy,implementing a control,observing operation and obtaining formal approval. A proposed owner is not an assignment to a real person. Where the task needs legal interpretation,qualified assessment or management acceptance,write the exact question and supporting facts for that reviewer.

## Exceptions and dates

Record who can accept an exception,its scope,rationale,conditions,expiry/review context and evidence. Do not grant it yourself or treat an unverified waiver comment as binding authority. When a deadline matters,verify the actual source,trigger,date basis and exceptions rather than copying a generic table. A plan may include an owner-approved target date without pretending it is a legal deadline.

## Closure

Reassess against the original criterion and relevant current scope. A closed ticket,uploaded file or changed configuration is not proof the intended control operated during the required period. Record actual retest evidence,remaining sampling/time limits and unresolved downstream effects. Keep superseded evidence and decision history according to the actual retention/hold policy.

Produce an executive-readable decision brief and a traceable detailed register. State draft/preparation status and the remaining reviewer/operational acceptance precisely. Do not replace requested useful analysis with generic disclaimers,but never assert an approval,certification or compliance determination that has not occurred.
