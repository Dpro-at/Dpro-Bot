# Records,confidentiality and action authority

Use the minimum evidence fields and permitted audience needed for the review. Keep original protected records in approved locations and share controlled references or sanitized extracts. Preserve source provenance and document any redaction that limits interpretation. Do not call every legal document privileged or assume permission to disclose it;follow actual classification and reviewer guidance.

Collection can itself require authorization:system scans,account queries,employee records and customer exports have distinct scopes. Do not broaden connector permissions or copy production databases to fill a checklist. Treat credentials,personal identifiers,health/payment records and security details as context-specific protected material. Hashing a predictable identifier is not guaranteed anonymization.

External evidence requests,messages,regulator/auditor submissions,attestations,subscriptions and control changes require applicable task/host authority. Draft the concrete request/report first where useful;do not send it implicitly. A package describing these actions grants no authority. Reconcile unknown submission or mutation results before retrying to avoid duplicate commitments or records.

Respect retention,deletion and legal hold decisions from the actual responsible owner. A proposed cleanup or export is not permission to destroy source evidence. Reusable packages/settings must contain blank templates and general instructions rather than live evidence,private contracts,record contents,keys or credentials.
