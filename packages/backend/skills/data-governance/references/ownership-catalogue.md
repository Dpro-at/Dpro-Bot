# Ownership, classification and catalogue records

## Responsibility

Identify who can define business meaning, approve access, operate storage, fix quality and accept an exception. One person can hold several roles, but a technical administrator is not automatically the owner of every business definition. Record the authoritative source of the assignment and how unresolved ownership will be decided.

Scope stewardship to a dataset/domain and the decisions it covers. A mailbox without an accountable response path is weak ownership evidence. Do not invent service-level commitments; use the actual internal agreement or propose one for review.

## Catalogue quality

A useful record identifies the dataset, source of record, version, grain, keys, purpose, approved uses, sensitivity, freshness, consumers, lineage coverage, operational owner and access process. Link authoritative definitions rather than copying stale versions into multiple catalogues. Keep private locations protected according to the existing workspace rules.

A dataset's title does not establish its contents or reliability. Record whether the entry is self-reported, inferred from metadata or verified against an observation. If a schema changed, retain the relevant version and identify which consumers need review.

## Classification

Use the approved categories. When none exist, propose an explicit taxonomy for approval instead of representing it as company policy. Determine whether classification applies to individual fields, the whole dataset, derived outputs or all of them. Consider whether several low-sensitivity fields become identifying in combination.

Pseudonymization, aggregation and masking have different properties. Do not label a dataset anonymous because obvious names were removed. An aggregate can still expose a very small group. State what was inspected and what remains unknown; do not copy private examples into a general skill asset.

## Glossary decisions

For ambiguous terms, capture each current definition, business context, owner and observed consumer. Resolve differences through the authorized owner rather than declaring one formula correct based on naming. Preserve scoped definitions when the organization legitimately uses distinct measures. A definition change can require migration and communication, not just editing a glossary cell.
