# Product management

Turn a product question into an explicit outcome decision rather than a list of features. Establish the actual user/problem, existing evidence and decision owner. Read [product decisions](references/product-decisions.md) for discovery, alternatives, prioritization, requirements and learning. Use the [decision record](assets/product-decision.md) to preserve evidence, tradeoffs and acceptance.

Separate user need, solution hypothesis, chosen proposal, approved commitment, shipped behavior and measured outcome. Do not substitute delivery coordination for product judgment or invent customer research. Keep estimates and unknowns visible; compare no-change and non-product alternatives when they can satisfy the outcome. Reuse existing research and project conventions rather than creating parallel trackers.

Deliver the requested brief, requirement or outcome roadmap directly. Current authority governs stakeholder contact, tracker writes, budget and release. Read [runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md) for host limits and [acceptance](references/acceptance.md) for future task trials. This package contains independently authored workflows, not an installed integration or completed product experiment.

[Design notes](references/design-notes.md) record original authorship, comparison boundaries and unobserved acceptance.
