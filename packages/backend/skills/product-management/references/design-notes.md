# Design notes

Original product-decision workflow connecting discovery evidence, no-change/process/product options, requirements and outcome learning. It complements research and delivery coordination; it does not supply stakeholder approval or a release integration. No external skill text or licensed adaptation was copied. Blank templates contain no private records. Normal, missing-capability and adversarial task briefs are future evaluation inputs; import/format checks and actual model, audience and live-action acceptance remain separate.
