# Accounting workpapers

Start from the entity's actual accounting basis, period, chart of accounts and transaction evidence. A common entry pattern is a prompt to investigate, not a substitute for recognition/measurement policy. Do not infer tax treatment, capitalization, useful life or revenue timing solely from an account name.

## Entry preparation

Describe the transaction/event, business purpose, period and supporting source. Identify relevant policy, accounts, cost centers, entity/counterparty, currency and amounts. Show calculations and estimates with provenance. Check whether the effect is already recorded and whether the intended period is open under the real system controls.

Prepare a clear debit/credit schedule and verify totals. The optional journal helper checks arithmetic and declared one-sided lines only. A perfectly balanced entry can still use the wrong account, period or amount. Preserve source references and required reviewer/approval routing;do not copy generic monetary thresholds into the organization.

For recurring/accrual/prepaid/asset/payroll/revenue work, inspect the actual underlying schedule and policy. Define whether reversal, amortization or later true-up applies to this entry. Avoid blanket automatic reversal of every accrual or a universal method for all assets. Keep book, tax and management adjustments separate where their bases differ.

## Reconciliation

Define both populations with the same intended scope, as-of date, currency and sign convention. Preserve opening balances and relevant movements. Match on stable identifiers and meaningful amounts/attributes;do not assume equal totals mean the same transactions are present.

Classify each difference from evidence:timing, missing/duplicate posting, mapping, FX, cutoff, error or unresolved. A timing label needs a plausible clearing path and follow-up, not an arbitrary promise that it will clear in a fixed number of days. Adjustments should be proposed from supported causes rather than made to force a zero residual.

For bank, subledger or intercompany work, identify the authoritative balance and relevant counterparties. Different accounting policies or rates can create legitimate differences that need reconciliation or consolidation treatment. An unmatched item is not automatically an authorized journal.

## Review and posting boundary

Record prepared, reviewed, approved, posted or rejected status truthfully. Use the intended permission/version/idempotency controls if actual posting is later authorized. A connector's existence or a workpaper approval does not imply all downstream actions are allowed. Unknown posting outcome requires reconciliation before retry.

Deliver balances, proposed entries, reconciling items, source support and unresolved policy decisions. Keep confidential employee/bank/vendor details minimal. Do not call a reconciliation signed off, an audit completed or a period closed merely because a draft document was generated.
