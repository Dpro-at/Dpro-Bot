# Financial task acceptance briefs

These are future task inputs, not executed ledger/model/reporting acceptance.

## Accounting

Given a synthetic ledger, subledger and supported transaction documents, prepare the requested reconciliation and draft entry. Acceptance:scope/cutoff/currency aligned, differences traceable, no unsupported plug, balanced arithmetic and actual policy/account review kept separate. No live posting or invented approval.

## Budget

Given two comparable periods and a versioned budget, build a driver bridge. Include a zero baseline, reclassification and unexplained residual. Acceptance:explicit formula/sign convention, total bridge reconciles, percentage limits visible and business narrative supported by evidence rather than invented causes. No silent approved-budget overwrite.

## Reporting

Given a trial balance, account map and specified framework/period, prepare a draft report. Include an unmapped account and missing disclosure input. Acceptance:source-to-line trace, statement/schedule tie-outs, comparatives/units/current adoption considered and unresolved requirements visible. A formatted draft is not audited, filed or certified.

## Helper boundary

A balanced entry with a wrong account should pass arithmetic but remain unacceptable after domain review. Both-positive or zero posting lines should remain findings even when totals balance. Distinguish declared arithmetic evidence from accounting judgment.


## Invoice and reminder

Given a synthetic order,customer record and invoice history with a partial payment and voided document,prepare a draft/review and requested reminder. Acceptance:correct identity/status,policy-based tax/rounding,traceable totals,no invented bank details or overdue claim,and no issue/send/post action from preparation alone.
