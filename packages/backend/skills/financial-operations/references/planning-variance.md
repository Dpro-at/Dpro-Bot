# Planning and variance

Define whether the task is a budget, forecast, scenario or comparison of actual results. Record entity, scope, time horizon, granularity, currency, version and owner assumptions. A forecast is conditional on assumptions;it is not an observed outcome or guaranteed financial result.

## Build a comparable baseline

Identify the approved budget/forecast version and actual source. Align periods, account mappings, entity scope, units, FX treatment and one-time/reclassification adjustments. Preserve both reported and comparable views when adjustments matter. Do not silently rewrite the baseline to make performance look better.

Define absolute and percentage variance conventions before computing. Explain favorable/unfavorable from the business measure, not just the sign. A cost increase and a revenue increase have different interpretations, and lower spending can reflect under-delivery. A zero or sign-changing baseline can make percentage change misleading;report a meaningful alternative or explicit limitation.

## Drivers and bridges

Choose an appropriate decomposition for the data and question:volume, rate, price, mix, capacity, timing, currency, new/discontinued items or another supported driver. Show formulas and the ordering/convention used when interaction terms exist. Reconcile component contributions to the total change and retain any residual;do not label a residual as an identified cause.

Separate arithmetic attribution from business causality. A price/volume bridge can explain a numerical change under a chosen method without proving why customers behaved differently. Use contracts, operating metrics or owner evidence for narratives. State unknown causes as questions rather than inventing a confident explanation.

## Scenarios

Make drivers, dependencies and assumptions explicit. Distinguish a base case, alternative scenario and sensitivity to one input. Avoid changing several hidden assumptions while calling the result a one-variable sensitivity. Include relevant capacity, cash timing or resource constraints instead of projecting every line independently.

Check totals, units, signs, formula ranges and expected simple/limiting cases. Preserve actual versus forecast labels and avoid false precision. Use the organization's supplied materiality/investigation policy or clearly mark a proposed decision;do not import a source's arbitrary thresholds.

## Delivery

Use the budget bridge to show baseline, actual/comparable outcome, driver contributions, residual and evidence. Separate findings, assumptions and actions requiring a decision. Do not update an approved budget, send a report or activate a planning service without the applicable authorization. A prepared model remains distinct from an approved operating plan.
