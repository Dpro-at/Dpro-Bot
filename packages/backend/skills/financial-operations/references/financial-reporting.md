# Financial reporting

Establish the report's actual basis and audience. Statutory financial statements, management packs, consolidation schedules and operational dashboards have different requirements. Confirm entity/group scope, period, comparatives, currency/unit, framework, adoption choices and relevant policy before choosing a format.

## Current requirements

Use authoritative current standards and entity policy for the reporting period. Do not combine US GAAP and IFRS rules into a generic universal checklist. Verify current classification, presentation, measurement and disclosure requirements for the specific transaction/entity when they matter. Do not claim compliance from a template alone.

For example, the IFRS Foundation states that [IFRS 18](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/) replaces IAS 1 and is effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, with earlier application permitted. Determine the entity's period/adoption position rather than labeling every 2026 or 2027 report identically. This narrow transition note is not a complete IFRS implementation guide;re-check the authoritative requirements during use.

## Map and tie

Map the source trial balance/ledger to report lines with stable account/category references. Identify unmapped accounts, duplicate inclusion, manual adjustments and elimination entries where relevant. Preserve account-to-line traceability and the reason for each material classification. A zero-sum trial balance does not establish complete disclosures or correct presentation.

Reconcile statements and supporting schedules using the applicable model. Check assets/liabilities/equity relationships, profit-to-equity movement and cash opening/movements/closing where appropriate. Confirm signs, comparative period, rounding and units. Do not fabricate a balancing cash flow or plug retained earnings to hide a missing movement.

Consolidation requires the actual scope, ownership, intercompany and accounting policy information. Do not infer eliminations or minority interests from a partial aggregate export. Keep missing source data and unresolved judgments visible.

## Notes and narrative

Support material claims and explanations with actual records. Distinguish audited/unaudited, draft/final and management-defined measures where relevant. Reconcile alternative measures to the intended authoritative basis when required by the actual reporting task. Avoid strengthening estimates into facts or describing an unexplained variance as a known operational event.

Review the complete delivered artifact, including tables, notes, cross-references and comparative labels. Formula/source checks, domain review, approval, filing and publication are separate states. Do not send or file a report merely because it is formatted. A preparation workflow does not issue an audit opinion, certify compliance or settle a disputed policy interpretation.
