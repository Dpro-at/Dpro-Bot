# Synthesis, evidence and inference

## Traceable findings

Preserve the chain from observation/source to coded pattern, interpretation and recommendation. Group by meaning that answers the research question, not merely by similar words. Keep disconfirming evidence and alternative explanations visible. A single strong failure can be important without becoming a claim that all users fail.

Separate participant statement, observed behavior, researcher inference and proposed design response. Do not label a generated persona or an expert walkthrough as participant evidence. If the source packet is incomplete, bound the conclusion to what was inspected.

## Numeric claims

For task measures, retain numerator/denominator, task/build/version, population, timing and assistance rules. Do not combine assisted and unassisted outcomes silently. Missing observations are not zero success or failure by default. A time-to-task value needs a consistent start/stop definition and treatment of incomplete tasks.

A frequency among a small purposive sample can describe that sample, not automatically the broader population. Avoid arbitrary confidence percentages or exact market claims from qualitative themes. For experiment/survey inference, inspect sampling, assignment, attrition and measurement before claiming a general effect.

## Priority and recommendations

Explain user impact, evidence strength, affected workflow and reversibility. Avoid a manufactured impact/effort score when the inputs are guesses. Recommend the smallest useful response or next check that addresses the supported problem. A usability issue may require content, structure, feedback or an underlying capability fix rather than a cosmetic redesign.

## Deliverable states

Distinguish planned research, recruited participants, completed sessions, inspected evidence, synthesis and validated design changes. Stakeholder agreement is not user validation; implementation is not outcome evidence. State the exact follow-up needed to learn whether the proposed fix works.

## Storage and sharing

Use the authorized protected location and permitted retention/sharing process. General reports can reference evidence without copying every transcript. Minimize personal/contextual details and do not export participant material into a portable skill package. Keep source limitations and unresolved ownership visible.
