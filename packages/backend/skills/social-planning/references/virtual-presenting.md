# Virtual presenting

## Purpose and evidence

Identify the presenter role,audience,communication objective,brand,tone,format and actual source material. Distinguish product explanation,training,news-style briefing and promotion. A synthetic presenter can deliver supported facts but cannot serve as independent customer proof or claim personal product use that did not occur.

Establish the exact approved script/claims,demo state,names,numbers,pronunciations and source versions. Use real product screens where demonstrating actual capability;label illustrative/proposed visuals. Preserve actual synthetic/commercial disclosure requirements for the platform/jurisdiction rather than assuming a profile label covers every placement.

## Presentation design

Choose a clear progression with useful context,concrete explanation/demonstration and appropriate next action. Coordinate spoken meaning with visible text,gesture,gaze,camera,product/UI and pacing. Do not overload the viewer with simultaneous unreadable captions,graphics and fast narration. Use pauses and visual holds where understanding requires them.

Reuse the synthetic identity record,script workflow and voice direction. Maintain continuity across takes while allowing intentional changes. A fixed prompt or voice name does not guarantee stable delivery or lip-sync. Prepare a complete teleprompter/shot/cue packet when generation cannot run;do not invent a finished avatar clip.

## Validation and delivery

For authorized production inspect actual pronunciation,claim accuracy,screen state,text/readability,gesture meaning,lip sync,crops,captions and disclosure. A rendered avatar file does not prove audience comprehension,credibility or factual accuracy. Record actual playback/listening scope and unavailable checks.

Deliver the packet or inspected media with script/source/identity versions,rights,actual review and remaining owner/platform acceptance. Paid generation,voice/likeness use,account changes,live broadcast and publication require actual authority. Keep private scripts/reference footage and credentials out of portable packages.
