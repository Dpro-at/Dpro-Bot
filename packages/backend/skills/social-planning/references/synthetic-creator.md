# Synthetic creator production

## Creative identity and truthful presentation

Define the fictional/synthetic concept,brand role,audience,channels,voice,visual direction and owner. Distinguish character fiction from factual product claims and actual personal experience. Do not invent customer testimonials,lived product use or independent endorsement for a generated person. Identify the disclosure needed for synthetic presentation and commercial relationships under the actual platform/jurisdiction;one profile bio may not be sufficient for every placement.

Use authorized assets and references. Record provenance,usage rights and the intended likeness boundary. A request for a fictional presenter does not require copying a real person's identity. Keep private reference media and consent records outside public/general skill exports. Do not imply the package itself created a real autonomous person or owns a social account.

## Continuity specification

Maintain one versioned character brief with stable visual/voice traits,allowed variations,wardrobe/props,brand tone and reference assets. Separate identity from scene,direction,camera,lighting and delivery constraints so a new brief does not silently redefine the character. Keep an approved reference set and record which model/tool/version/settings and inputs produced each output.

A deterministic prompt assembly only controls text inputs. Seeds,hex colors and reference conditioning do not guarantee the same face,body,voice or behavior across models,versions,shots and motion. Inspect actual outputs against the approved traits and intended use;record visible drift and bounded corrections rather than claiming identity lock from a schema.

## Production and review

Prepare a storyboard/script,shot purpose,visual action,spoken content,product/reference requirements,format and accessibility/disclosure placement. Prefer a manageable test asset before a costly batch. Generation,voice synthesis,model downloads and paid APIs follow actual host/cost authority. When unavailable,deliver full production-ready briefs without inventing rendered media.

Inspect generated images and video for continuity,product/text accuracy,anatomy,motion,voice/lip sync,crops,rights and disclosure readability. A successful API job is not visual acceptance. Avoid repeated full-batch generation when one asset needs repair. Preserve accepted revisions and record actual errors and retry/cost boundaries.

## Publishing and measurement

Use the existing calendar and actual account identity. Approved concept,approved asset,scheduled post,published content and audience reaction are separate states. Do not publish,engage with members,create accounts or represent sponsorship commitments from a production brief alone. Reconcile uncertain platform outcomes before retrying.

Measure actual reach/participation/outcome under defined source/window and account for synthetic presentation and paid distribution. Do not claim commercial lift,authentic consumer testimony or identity consistency from prompt quality alone. Deliver the continuity record,usable scripts/briefs or actual inspected assets,disclosure decisions and remaining generation/platform acceptance.
