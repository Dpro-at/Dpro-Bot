# Virtual acting

## Role and scene

Establish fictional character,scene objective,relationship,stakes,context,style and audience. Read approved script/character references and maintain actual rights/consent boundaries. Separate fictional characterization from real-person likeness and factual testimony. Do not invent a real performance,endorsement or lived experience for a synthetic character.

Break the scene into playable beats:what the character wants,obstacle,action/subtext,reaction and change. Specify delivery through concrete gaze,gesture,posture,facial/voice behavior and timing where it matters. Avoid reducing acting to generic emotional adjectives or stereotypes. Keep scene/line/take IDs and mark intentional continuity changes.

## Continuity and production

Reuse the versioned synthetic-creator record and approved references. Track script,appearance,wardrobe,props,screen direction,eyeline,voice and scene entry/exit state. Prompt consistency controls inputs but cannot guarantee generated anatomy,identity,emotion or lip sync. Inspect the actual take rather than declaring a deterministic identity lock.

Prepare a complete performance/shot brief when generation is unavailable. For authorized production,test a representative beat before a costly scene batch. Check actual model/tool capabilities and reference handling without importing paid-provider assumptions or unapproved voice/likeness cloning. Preserve accepted revisions and bound retries to actual authority/cost scope.

## Review and handoff

Evaluate the actual performance against intention,readability,continuity,interaction,timing,voice/lip sync and product/factual boundaries. A storyboard is not an acted scene;an accepted generation job is not a reviewed take. Record which frames/playback/audio were actually observed.

Deliver the useful role/beat packet or inspected media with rights/reference/version and remaining editor/owner acceptance. Reuse visual/audio workflows for finishing. Do not create accounts,contact actors or publish from a preparation request. Private character/reference media stays outside reusable packages.
